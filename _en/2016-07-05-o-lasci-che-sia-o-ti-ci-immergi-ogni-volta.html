Either you let it be or you plunge into it every time. You clench your teeth until you crush them, or you turn your eyes towards a smile. But what is inside a smile? What is enough to soothe weeping, to savour the waiting, to be certain it is only until we meet again? When we see each other again the past will be only an instant, I repeat every time, and I am certain of it. But who will say it to children without the strength of a dad? Children who will become men all the same, mysteriously, while I am elsewhere. And to think that an instant is enough.
