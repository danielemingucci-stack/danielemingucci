Or, giving up on steering communication in order to follow tastes and impressions (notes)

We are talking about branding and graphics, slogans, copy, art direction and so on. The instinct, the worries, the needs, the ideas, the tastes of the clients impose compromises, and it is almost always their demands that prevail. An acceptable “visual” compromise is found, and off we go. Does it work? Everything works, but we have to look carefully at what the goals are and what horizon we have in mind. What is at stake is always the “usual” question: either a brand is born or it is not.

OK, now we would need a long dissertation to decide what a brand is: it certainly does not come down to name-logo-colours-style. That is not all there is: seen from an operational angle, it is a process of building an identity (recognisable, distinct, characterised, evocative…) that takes shape and unfolds over time through a myriad of tools and details. That is: it has no immediate, instant link with the business. It has a horizon - in time, but not only - different from that of business development. This implies that the building process is different too, with feedback on the choices made (from the small day-by-day ones to the broader ones of medium-to-long-term strategy) arriving at times and in ways different from those of the business. It is a perspective in which the “gut” and instinct can enter the game, but cannot entirely define it, the game. Because the business is the spark, the source, the litmus test, but the business works even without the brand. There: you have to want the brand, you have to be convinced it is needed, you have to decide to tend it with a process parallel to that of the business, because it is another business, a supporting business, a synergic business. It can make the business easier, it can sustain it, it can help it through crises or multiply its potential for growth. But always on a different level.

So the gut yes, but not only that. The demands, ideas and instinct of the direct players always at the centre, but… if they remain the only horizon, there are consequences. Not today and not tomorrow morning, but there are: either the brand is born or it is not.

Yes, I am writing this because here, among us, the Italian excellences almost never work on a connection between brand values and the tools of relationship. I always think of Nike and Sergio Tacchini. But above all of the corrections and adaptations imposed by authority on content, both text and graphics, in the name of the (certainly reasonable) demands and needs of whoever runs the business. Too often they are Pyrrhic victories. And it would merely be frustrating if it did not have a tragic backdrop: the not-being-born of brands, of marks, of relationships. Of possibilities. But I have an age, a life, and “if you don’t want it, go without” (you, not me :). A pity that so many go without, because the business may well work, but then the trace left behind is far less substantial, the values stay blurred and in the end it is only money… a so-so story, at least to tell.
