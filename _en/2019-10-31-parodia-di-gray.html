I have studied many times the headstone they carved for you
The standard one, with boat, harbour and lowered sails
And it is the reality of a life in which departure and destination coincided.
Yes, mummy had so many problems and daddy was always there, on the sofa
But the years went by, the muscles swelled, the brain filled up with things.
And with wonders.
None of this pushed you to ease a hawser, to haul in a line…
Nor did you learn the mistake, you basked far away
From the madness of those who seek a meaning, your head firmly fixed in the hole you dug.
Here you are at last where you slowly wanted to be, in your deathly Eden.
Fear without desire left you always in the same harbour
Drowning you in words you never managed to fill with meaning.

Farewell
