The balance sheet is clear, one look is enough. A small sapling, and another that will not grow all that much. On the lawn where I live, seeds are thrown every day and each has its own story, a short one for the most part. It settles on the ground, sinks slowly down and finds a little of that warmth which allows it to transform, to die, they say, and to become the beginning of something. A small stem, a tiny leaf, a flower, then… Every day, seed after seed, with gladness, with hope, unwillingly sometimes, but one day after another the lawn where I live is flooded with this endless flow of seed that is given to me.

People look at me, they smile because I look like a child, I look like a fool (they do not say so, but you can see they think it), and perhaps I spread the cheerfulness of naivety, above all when I show my sowing to newcomers, people who do not know me yet and who are amazed, with no balance sheets, no judgements. They look at the flowers, they look at the seeds, how many are still left or are being born, then the sapling, then the sturdier one…

But the balance sheet - for anyone who can take a step back and pass judgement - has only that sapling that is solid, and perhaps the other one. There is no getting away from it, you do not need to be a genius to see it. And I can say nothing, I cannot defend myself from this because I have no excuse: each of us sees what we look at, and the saplings are small and unsure. Whoever lives with me, though, lives beside a lawn that every day blooms in a thousand colours. They are only flowers, but they are born every day. Those are what I see.

There are seeds that give rise to great trees that cross the years, the decades, the centuries. Others give rise to flowers that die in a day, and it seems to be nothing. But who really knows what will be born when they throw a seed? The lawn where I live is always in flower, and that is something. It is my life, it is a lawn full of colours.
