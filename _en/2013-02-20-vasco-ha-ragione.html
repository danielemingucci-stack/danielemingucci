The day advances and carries you off. Time literally beats you, and you do not even begin what you have in your head. You are stuck among the breakers and you get lost because you swim panting without making headway. I go to bed before half past eleven or after: it is the same. Sudden ideas appear and become anxieties in an instant. They are what you will not do, what you will not achieve, your new failures that will measure my smallness. I try to tear myself away from everything that holds me still, but it is life itself, and I will never take the step. Will I regret it? Every time I fear so… The boat, the web, the club, the magazine, the game, the site, the currency, the friend… Everything remains unfinished - not even imperfect - and will not get done.
I no longer even have the courage to switch on the light and pick up the Moleskine to note down the details. It is cold, she is asleep and the child is asleep…. anyway, it will not get done.
I dream of going to America where, over there, anything can happen! But I have been there, and the timescales of success are not as magical as they tell it.
Vasco is right, you have to be ready for anything after half past eleven at night. I am writing this at six, when in winter it is neither night nor day. I have an idea that tomorrow…. It will not get done.
