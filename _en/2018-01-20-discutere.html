“Today in Matera innovation is being discussed…”.

R. Bonacina

I am curious: how do you discuss innovation, what do you say? I can understand discussing an innovation, but on the concept itself I struggle to imagine. Come and see, I will be told, proving that perhaps I am not curious enough. It is that I prefer to imagine it, an animated discussion on providing tools to innovators (but what kind of innovators are they if it is others who give them tools?), creating “ecosystems” (but is an ecosystem planned on paper not a contradiction in terms?), activating policies (yeah, right)… Or the hubs, the legendary hubs where everyone is connected, everyone contributes, but it is not clear who makes money (someone always makes money, mind).

My instinct is to say that innovations are made, not discussed. And they should be made when they are needed, following an intuition, working away at a problem… There, problems really can be discussed, and it is certainly very useful to do so, because ideas, new solutions, can be born from discussion too (but then, or first, you have to do research, experiment, not just brainstorm)…
