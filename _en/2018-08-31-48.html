We are surfers waiting for the perfect wave

we ride them all, one after another,

not for where they will take us but for their beauty.

We have wetsuits by now worn out

eaten by salt and sun they do not stop us

feeling the warmth of the sea and savouring its embrace.

We are surfers in fast waiting

we scan the horizon and the movements of the sky

we let ourselves be carried by unknown currents

chatting about our adventures.

We are at sea, brother, perhaps too close to the shore,

we are travelling and we will not stop.

Happy birthday, Simone.
