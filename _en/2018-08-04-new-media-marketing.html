When I see big companies throw themselves into new media or up-to-date marketing strategies, I think that times have finally changed and that not everything is as immobile as it used to be. Going into the substance, though, the news always deflates, because once the limelight is switched off and the dazzling and often real possibilities opened up by the new approaches are set aside, we always come back to content, to the relationship with the customer, to “reality”. To play it down, I could say it is a bit as if they announced to me that they had changed car and got a very fast, very modern, very efficient one. Fantastic, but we know perfectly well that in three years at most it will need changing (unless one is willing to make do, obviously) and that the theme remains the journey anyway: yes, lovely car, but where are you going?

The same goes for innovative business strategies, for work on mentality, on motivation, on company organisation and so on. They are important things and above all they are effective. They are fundamental, all the more so, for organisations that at bottom always do the same thing, the same business in the same market, with the same strengths and so on. I do not want to flatten things, but… variety helps to beat monotony.

The sky lights up with comets and meteors, flashes of light one after another: some have the energy to stay lit for a long time, some burn it all in an instant of perfect light. But the stars? Which are the stars?

This is not a criticism, nor an attack on new media, heaven forbid. Only, I believe that when language and reality are involved, long-term investments should have foundations more solid than trends. Shining is easy, after all; it is enough to burn, and anything burns. The quality of the fuel, how long it lasts, its ability to renew itself - and, staying within the metaphor, not to pollute - are fundamental elements for being stars. You need to look inside yourself before communicating, to work on processes, on the whole truth of your company: that is what growth is. Expressing a vision, sharing it from the beginning to the end of the process, improving the quality kind, from the people who produce it to those who consume it. A reflection on meaning is needed, always, every time, for something long-term. Otherwise, how will employees and consumers be able to trust: will we go on for ever shoving one season’s success in their faces?

14/12/2016
