She came up to me, told me she was interested. It had struck her somehow, my story, the four things I had lined up. And a journey began, silent, adventurous. No challenge, no heroism, a tide of things, of small gestures in the midst of big things. Co-opera d’arte, a work of art made together, sharing the heart… she lit sparks and started fires in the middle of the forest of ideas and things, various, vague, imperfect. She was simply one of the greats, everyone knows, who set off, on the fly, at once, without a second thought and with no fear. But I… I have a story, I had one, a beautiful, gripping one, and that day it changed, it accelerated, perhaps it even went off like a bomb. Oxygen came in, the heart was flung wide open, I became Crazy Horse. Best wishes, crazy horse, she wrote to me. The world became bigger, the heart became more capable, braver, the mystery became true, human, infinite. Homo viator, she, and I learned. I used to do things I was asked to do, then I did one on my own initiative, and at the end of that first journey came the gift of her way of looking at things, the world, life. An imperfect gaze? A certainty different from the one I knew, things my heart believed impossible, unseeable, not real. In a row, one after another, journeys, encounters, faces… who knows. I do not know, I really do not know, and the privilege of shared moments, questions, visions, dreams, paradise. Do you wear glasses? Only on Fridays. Remember your promise. Which one? To let me come aboard. My God, my God… and now? I will take you there, somehow I will take you there, onto that sea which is teaching me not even to imagine that on my own I… You know. And I told you, shyly, what meeting you all has been. I address you as “you all”, as “those ones”. And I swear, we will see each other as soon as I can.
