A horrible title, but… it is exactly the point. I could have tried “Getting cultured in order to react”, but the link between reacting and reactionaries is not transparent to everyone. So I will try to get into the substance and try to be content with that. Culture is one of the fundamental dimensions of a man’s life. Together with affection, work and politics it is one of the decisive factors for the full realisation of a life, for a presence in the times in which each of us is immersed. And it is the most mistreated of these, because it is the one that gives the least immediate satisfactions; it is tiring, complex, changeable, it offers no ready to play advantages. Most people keep away from it, take what comes, keep what they have, content with the bare minimum. This approach, this inertia, has never been all right with me: I consider it irresponsible, stupidly passive.

But that is not all, because there are many culturally committed people, and among these the main exercise is criticism. Which is, after all, a fundamental aspect of culture, of making culture: building a judgement, an opinion, recognising links and then… choosing?

I think krinein is the Greek verb: it means both to cut and to judge. Criticism comes from there, you cut and then… this side or that. It is not enough for me. It is even more passive than those who do not care and content themselves with other things. It is Manichaean, Sherlock Holmes stuff, right or wrong, good guys and bad guys: the investigator discovers the murderer (in the best of cases), but does not save the victim nor console the loved ones… For culture that is not enough! Discovering what is behind, what is underneath, what the “danger” is, the “threat”, the “poison”, is only one aspect, a part that is not always useful, most of the time paralysing. You close the newspaper, the magazine, the web page on the tablet, you settle comfortably on the sofa with a smug little smile: “caught them again this time…”. That is not culture, it is a cemetery in the living room.

The ability to dig, to recognise links, problems, shortcomings, lacks something fundamental: it lacks life, it lacks the ability to create deep relationships (relationships based on sharing judgements last until the next judgement, no longer), to venture into building, into taking risks together. That is not culture, it is puzzle-solving, an intellectual exercise. It is a part, and not even the most beautiful.

Yes, because whoever spends themselves with this strategy becomes the victim of that cut (krinein): they have culture, but they do not make any. They undergo things, they stay a step behind, and their life is split in two: what they understand, what they are forced to live. They have no effect on their surroundings except through opinions, but they are not able to bring three people together and build something. They do not generate an enthusiasm capable of building, of bringing together, of taking risks. Having a judgement is something, but it is not enough.

I once heard it said that truly giving a judgement is saying “yes” to reality, immersing yourself in what you have in front of you, putting energy, strength, ideas into it. There, that corresponds to me: going, throwing yourself in, taking risks. On the run, running, it is important to say here, there, at every bend, but it is anything but doing so sitting down. Because when you decide while running, at the next step something happens.

The idea I am forming is that at the base there is something moral: a decision at the outset about what one truly desires. To destroy or to build… but it is not an explanation that satisfies me, because we are back to good guys and bad guys, and above all it does not seem possible to change things. I will reflect on it further, but meanwhile I want to say one thing: there is too much plastic, too much rubbish, too much pollution, waste, air conditioning and so on. Greta or no Greta, conspiracy or no conspiracy, we can do better, much better, and I do not see why we should not try.
