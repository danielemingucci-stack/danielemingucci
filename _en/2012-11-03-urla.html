Something screams, almost shrieks, between my temples. Music, perhaps words that might open my heart at least a little? No. It no longer beats, my heart; my temples throb and wrath gains ground among my limbs, weary with nothing. It screams, almost screeches between my burning temples, the bored or tired lust - one no longer knows the difference - of a time that no longer knows how to have peace. A bothersome unease gains ground, unworthy to be called pain: something is urgent. Perhaps a stroke of fortune that might free me from everything that makes me feel like a slave.
