Today, the beginning of my goodbye to LinkedIn… (wonderful to link to it in order to leave it :)

The last post:

The algorithm does not reward those who post little

I publish when I want to say something that matters to me. But evidently I publish too little, in the eyes of the algorithm. Which does not reward those who publish little. Damn.

The thing is, I do other things, and if every now and then I have something to tell, it is precisely because I do other things. In short, even the algorithm - on LinkedIn - makes me anxious, and I do not like anxiety. The other anxiety comes to me, inexplicably, from the triumphant marvels of successes following one after another, post after post… but that is probably my problem :)

In short, as soon as I have a moment I think I will clear everything out of here, empty it and leave only the contacts. I have a blog, somewhere, perhaps we will see each other there. Held in check by a Microsoft algorithm, I do not feel at ease :)
