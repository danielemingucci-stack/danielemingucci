We dirtied our mouths for at least two decades declaring our unconditional love for culture. We complained about everything, from closed doors to scant economic means, circling in many ways around a single concept: we need a space that we cannot find.
All that is over. None of it is true any more. It was tall tales, lies, excuses and nonsense… Today we have that space, today there is all the space we want, but we have stopped. We deserved everything, then; we got what we were really worthy of. Nothing.
Culture is relationship. Culture carries within itself, in its own name, the sense of cultivating and of gathering. Relationships and occasions to cultivate and to seize: working, in a word. Using time and making it flower. Never mind introspection, never mind research: depth and discovery are fruits of relationship and of occasions. Encounters and rapport.
God save ilbukowski.
