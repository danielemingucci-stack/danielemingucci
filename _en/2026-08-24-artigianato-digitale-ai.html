With AI tools it now seems we can manage on our own, with results that are pleasing to the eye and sometimes even excellent. There is, however, no real process of distinction and characterisation; we flatten ourselves - paradoxically - onto big aesthetic wows. But the wow effect is only one of the factors in play, because the process of building and maintaining an identity is highly articulated and complex. Unconscious too, in some respects, but deeply human. A graphic designer, an art director, whether working in continuity or in discontinuity, interprets a flow and opens a cycle. They inscribe their action in an articulated and deep relationship with the players, with history, with trends, with visions… AI does not work quite like that, not always, not without a complex set-up process that is fine-tuned only in the rarest cases. Will it evolve? Perhaps. Not because it is unable to, but because it may have no interest in doing so: large centralised systems have an innate vocation to make things uniform and flat. Fine. What do I think will happen? We will lose a generation of graphic designers and art directors, copywriters and content creators; a break in continuity will open up, filled with AI gimmicks more or less cheap, easy, dazzling… and we will all become the same, like 99% of the blessed posts we put up with on social media. With this cut in the middle, in a while we will have to start again almost from scratch, and we will have lost a great deal. Of talent, of inventiveness, of humanity and - here I skip a few steps - of meaning. That said, this was not Nostradamus’s corner; it was only to say that AI used by the crafty will probably bounce us to where we would rather not go… in communication, and perhaps in a great many other fields too.
