The old woman leans out, pushing the curtains aside, because she heard a thud on her balcony. But the boy is already on the floor below: he sits on the railing, reaches down with one hand, then the other, a jump into the void, a light tug on the arms, and then he lets go - at the right moment - after swinging a few degrees to throw himself onto the balcony of the next floor down.

Fourth floor, third, second, first, the lady leaning out and the last leap - feet together - onto the windscreen of the traffic wardens' car parked under the building. God bless parkour. And the clueless wardens who went up as a pair without leaving anyone on guard.

He jumps on the scooter at full speed, loosens the backpack a little on his shoulders and takes the roundabout the wrong way, at the end of the avenue. Mistake, they aren't following him, no need to do stupid shit and get noticed.

It's absurd to have to set up a mess like this just for a change to the Mac's Finder, but it's also a lot of fun: now it looks like a Netflix series, with wiretaps and chases. In Riccione. Wrong setting, definitely. Traffic wardens instead of the FBI and mum who... oh god, she's already calling. He doesn't answer.

The Mac has turned into Windows, File Manager pops up everywhere! On the iPad finding a file is a treasure hunt... all stuff conceived before the internet! They've piled apps on top of it, one after another, they only give room to whoever they like and... basically they've smoked away the whole beauty of having a desktop.

He's almost at the sea, turns into a deserted avenue with all the hotels closed and the doors barred for winter, stops behind the trunk of a big plane tree and takes off backpack and hoodie. He turns it inside out and as if by magic it goes from red to black, puts the backpack back on and heads straight for the cycle path along the beach. The phone vibrates again. An anonymous ring. Twenty seconds and the private number again: “Got everything?”, “Yes, everything on my laptop”, “Good, see you at the colony, don't get followed”. The scooter is one of the rental ones, hacked, unlocked, GPS disconnected. He leaves it hidden behind a newsstand, that too closed for winter, and carries on on foot while slipping the phone into the special pocket, lined all over with tin foil.

The sea is grey and melts into a sky of the same colour. Even the San Bartolo headland is blurred, but from up here everything is beautiful all the same. The abandoned holiday colony on the beach is a sumptuous heap of rubble. The blue tiles that cover it have shades that create a wonderful effect next to the sea, especially when the sun is out. Today it isn't, but the two friends are inside anyway, not outside. On the top floor, where one room has stayed almost intact and they've built their base. Crazy, for fun, at first: four solar panels nicked from a building site, the control unit, the batteries, a satellite dish for the internet: little power, just enough to charge the devices and connect anonymously to wonderland: the deep web. And put a team together.

“What time did they come?”, “Half an hour ago, I told you”, “And they didn't say anything?”, “They asked if I was home, what do you think they were looking for?”, “You didn't open and...?”, “My mum was in the bath, I grabbed everything and ran down from the terrace”. Did they follow him?

Do they know about the colony? It's bloody freezing, where the fuck do we go... “Come on, let's plug everything in”. It's already a test, the team works together on the new system, beta version. The Mac boots and so does the iPad. They're connected, the boy stops the normal startup with a key combination, a screen of code appears, he types one line, two, ten. He hits enter and the beta starts. The apple is no longer bitten, it spins on itself then splits into wedges and all together they become a flower: OS Flower - The new era, together. The boy still has his doubt: he would have written connected instead of together, but the group voted as one. The desktop opens, Mac HD appears, the folders and so on and so on. He opens the one called Work in Pro and smiles. It's always a thrill when it works, even though it has always worked for a few months now: inside there aren't just the files, there's also the whole timeline of messages (on the right) and the report of changes (at the bottom), the general one with the most important things and the analytical one, on request, with every single detail.

His friend has booted everything too and he sees him connected, again in the message timeline. Crack.

It's a noise, on the floor below. Heartbeats stop. Three seconds, then slowly, in absolute silence, they close everything and fill the backpacks. They listen, move in slow motion, stay still a little longer, with a movement of the eyes they decide on the “emergency” exit (onto the roof, then down towards the beach by the crumbling staircase) and approach the window with the caution of a cat.

“Sir, we only wanted...”, “Don't call me sir, come on!”, and what the fuck do I call this old guy dressed like a Man in Black? “We only wanted to run some tests and then...” shit on that shitty multinational that doesn't pay taxes and hasn't made a decent computer in ten years, my old man says (actually he says twenty). “You see...”, “You can drop the formalities, calm down, we're just chatting”, “Yes, well, it's a bit...”, will he get pissed off if I say Eighties? How do I explain myself, “... a bit stuck for a while now, the Mac, and we thought that...”. Am I confessing to something?

Is he a lawyer, a detective? He saved my arse from the Police, but... “Are you recording what I say?”, “No, relax. I'm on your side, go on”. The “creature” takes over, in the end: talking about it excites him, he sees it in front of him, he says in no particular order all the things they've thought of, how they've organised them, why they're better, how easy it will be to share what you do, what you invent, what you create... “When they made the first Mac, there was no internet, no email, no texts, no cloud. All these things were added onto the original structure, put one on top of the other, without changing it. Now you find yourself with an archive of documents on the desktop, another archive with the same folders in your email, a list of messages from the same people you deal with for the same jobs somewhere else again, the cloud, the shared folders...”. “Well, there are the Slacks and things like that, aren't there?”, “But those are just the same mess again, another place to go looking for things, messages, attachments, file versions. In the end they're messed-up Whatsapps, aren't they?”. In fact, you always end up using Whatsapp... or email, depending on your age. “Skype, Teams, Zoom... chats, archives, attachments... I'm here, you're there, wait, send it again, are you sure it's the right version?”. They look at each other, the man stays silent. He wants the ending. The boy has a moment of uncertainty, thinks of his friend: have they caught him? Did he manage to hide? Is he looking for him?

“Do you want something else to drink? I think a hot tea would do you a world of good... meanwhile, do you want to use my phone?”. Another hesitation, no. Better not. “Go on, please. How did you put all this mess in order?”. A flower! “A flower is always a flower. In reality the petals of a flower change, fall, new ones grow, they turn with the sun, with the wind. A flower instead of a folder: one stem, many petals. You open the folder - yes, the folders are still there, it's still a desktop after all - and inside are your files (on the left), the ones you share with the team (on the right) - they're together, but clearly distinct and you can move them from here to there - and further right still there's the team you message with (we gather messages from every app, Whatsapp, Telegram, your Slack, Lark...) and below that the emails, with the same logic”. “Explain the reports to me”.

Totally awesome! “When you've been away for a while, the system catches you up without you having to reread everything: main things, automatically, like in... you know the iPhone photo albums that every now and then make you a collection of photos edited into a video? It works more or less like that... and if you want to see the details you click on analytics and you see everything”. Meanwhile he's taken his phone out of the backpack. It rings. Huge fuck-up. An exchange of glances: “Follow me” says the man, and they run out.

“Criminal conspiracy aimed at... what is your association aimed at?”. How the fuck does this dickhead talk? “Criminal? What are you saying, we only...”, “When one or more persons join together and organise themselves outside the law, that constitutes the offence of criminal conspiracy. Which is serious, very serious, if you haven't understood yet, my boy. Now... aimed at what, this association? We saw there were two of you and after we've looked into your computer…”. Does this guy work for Apple? Do the Carabinieri of Riccione now work for an American multinational? He holds back a flash of irritation and takes a breath: “We're not criminals. It was an... experiment”. Criminal conspiracy, kids from all over the world getting together to make something work that hasn't worked for years (yes, everyone pretends it works, but fuck, it goes in slow motion, it's like a ministry!)... criminal conspiracy.

When he finds out we're scattered across five continents I'll end up like Pablo Escobar. “Monopolies, it's always a fight for the monopoly and you can't touch anything. We're just a group of kids trying to change things and annoying the monopolist giants...”, “So you're a subversive group”, the officer cuts in. This one really is stupid, then. He shuts his mouth, says nothing more. Two hours later, face like thunder, dad gets him out. He doesn't say anything either, straight home, no going out, no communicating.

Will Smith is driving, very fast, but he seems relaxed, he's in full control. “In the end it's like Teams, nothing new…”. Like hell it's like Teams. For a start it's free and then… we think the other way round. Include, not exclude and force everyone into a single system so you can charge… “OS Flower gathers messages from every platform. Then it makes the reports… it's another world. You don't have to get in, you're in, with your privacy, your spaces, and what you want to share, update… a second, a flight. The meadows fill with flowers, for free, and the flowers colour the world”.

The man thinks of the Dutch Tulip bubble, it's a conditioned reflex. Nothing is free, ever… that's how he reasons, there's no other way, life has taught him. Then the bang, a terrible crash, the car out of control, sparks, everywhere, the world spinning, the airbags exploding, the dull noise of metal scraping on asphalt, they slow down, they stop. No blood, a stink of firecrackers and burning - the gunpowder from the airbags - a little nausea rising and two hands grabbing the boy through the blown-out windows. The man, beside him, seems unconscious but starts to move, on the other side the boy recognises a friendly face. It's the Indian who wrote the messaging algorithm: “Are you ok? Ehi, is it ok? Friends, we're your friends”, they lift him and pull him out. He's out, standing, he leans into the car to grab the backpack, meets the man's eyes: “It should be simpler than this. Everything. At least trying should be easy”. They're free, they leave.

OS Flower is just an idea, not even a project, but it's a flower we have a right to. At least to try planting it, to see its colours and let ideas, relationships, working groups bloom. The net belongs to everyone, the net must run free…
