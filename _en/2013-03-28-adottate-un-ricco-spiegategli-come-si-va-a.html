“Adopt a rich man. Explain to him how to go for a walk, how to savour a landscape, how to enter into dialogue with it”. In short: make him fall for it and convince him to collaborate, to lend a hand. In full awareness, though. Which is not that of the mite given out of charity, but that of knowing one belongs to a common land, of which we feel part and to which we must give back".

Philippe Daverio
