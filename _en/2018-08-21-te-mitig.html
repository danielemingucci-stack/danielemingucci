They are certainly contributing causes, and more than anything one could look to the low passes of vultures, be they poets or wheeler-dealers. But the Rimini Meeting has something inverted about it, a basic malfunction well masked by the extraordinary human material and by people’s contagious enthusiasm. The location: perhaps it is simply the location that is not suitable. Everything is organised in a scattered way, big spaces for what is not needed, tiny ones for the most interesting things. It is hard even to order a sandwich, but then already at the entrance you go out where people would come in and you come in, obviously, by a little side road. Above all, talent: that is hidden systematically, almost stubbornly. There are no risks: no expressive risks, no architectural risks; no messages emerge, no personalities, no talents, precisely. Beautiful illustrations with shining eyes hidden in a small stand on the edge, enormous spaces for a strange, incomprehensible labyrinth. Senseless, with no route through it. The shows themselves, perhaps very beautiful, seem built, hosted, conceived as a filler, always on the same clichés: what shall we do this year? People look for something to say in order to say it, to keep a space, without understanding that this is exactly how you relegate yourself to an unsuitable space - Rimini is no longer suited to the Meeting, not that absurd exhibition centre without a centre - when the reasonable path would be to look for spaces in order to say something. You breathe a tiredness, well compensated as I said by so many fine people, but all a little lost, out of place. Precisely because the denial of talent prevents each from being in their own place, so - really very charmingly - a surgeon serves piadina, a student in his Sunday best escorts a leading personality. Nobody is at their best, and this is strategically intended: as if living inside a parenthesis, one accepts the sacrifice of giving oneself, one’s time and one’s patience (which is the capacity to suffer, let us not forget), curious about the fruits it will bear. But it cannot be a good strategy, not for challenging the world on the front of culture, of the vision of reality, of ideal drive. It only creates a small riverbed declared miraculous in advance, in which a few tired talents (always the same ones) seem more occupied with keeping a leadership than with truly meeting. Because a real encounter is a risk, yes, and has one terrible characteristic: it changes things, it calls everything into question. It forces you to shift, move, scheme, give way without organising generational handovers… And this last is perhaps “the” invincible taboo: the spontaneity of the original atmosphere has to be ritually recreated every year, because it is the only obligatory certainty of a protected environment. The meeting is a miracle, by definition, even before it has taken place. For someone like me it is exhausting; for others it is evidently reassuring; but carrying on like this, shut up in jargon and habit, it will be ever harder to talk with a world that runs. People will content themselves with a selfie with one of the right ones, to put among the trading cards of the “good guys” and to have had their quarter-hour of attention (not even of fame…). Yes, because even the encounters that are very beautiful get burned and digested quickly, perhaps leaving something to the élites, but for ordinary people - and above all for mass culture, pardon, the culture of the people - they leave almost zero. And so, “to do what”?
