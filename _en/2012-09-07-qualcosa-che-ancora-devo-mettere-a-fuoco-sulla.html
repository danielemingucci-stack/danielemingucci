There is something that has always made me uneasy about poetry gatherings, or festivals, or contests, or collective readings. I think it is a sense of intimacy or even of modesty violated, an underlying obscenity. Like a betrayal of the why of poetry. I live it as a means to something, towards something. Made a theme in itself, it seems to me to become mania at once, to be no longer itself. A form in the service of a thing - of a meaning, a content, an emotion… - which is instead made a theme in itself. Well, it seems to me to lose significance, to empty itself completely of meaning. Who is listening to you there, meanwhile? Experts, other poets, critics. For whom did you write? For them? I do not know…

Which is to say that the passion for poetry in itself has something worm-eaten about it; it is like being passionate about love without loving anyone. Poetry, like love, is an accident, a crash: it serves the heart or it serves nothing; it is for the soul… It cannot be for the experts, for the poets, for the good ones, for those who understand… It is an obscenity, at bottom, of which I can justify only the great passion and care, but which searches in the wrong place and, in the end, distances us from humanity and from a real confrontation with reality.

How many take part in these gatherings? How many are in front of the TV instead? I remember a bitter Eugenio Scalfari sending back to Fazio his compliments on the 50 thousand copies sold of a book: “50 thousand are nothing”, he said…

Now, what are they waiting for to bear fruit, all these poets who talk among themselves and understand one another? Let them be more intimate, let them ask a few friends (as few as possible) for help in refining the form. Then let them begin a confrontation with a world that no longer wants them, no longer understands them, has never loved them. Let them begin, in a spirit of service, to steal from us a: “how well he put it! How he managed to catch in an instant the heart of the thing!”… If only they would make themselves loved by as many people as possible….
