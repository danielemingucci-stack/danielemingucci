There is a third sector of little men and little women who help, lend a hand, solve and ease things with small jobs and odd jobs, down-to-earth solutions, wise ones and above all fair ones. Off the books, mostly. All wrong: the reduction to little men and little women, the mention of off the books… because they are giants, in heart and experience, in skill and in the way they look at things, in curiosity and appetite for life, builders of a simple, close-at-hand beauty. An honest one. They take little more than expenses, less than what the call-out alone (as it appears on an invoice) would cost you, often to solve nothing at all. They top up their income, they set up small businesses, true, which keep them alive and lift them out of the niche of the useless and kept, too close to the poverty line. Alive, transmitters of life, of wisdom, of skill and knowledge… handers-down of lost arts and lost ways of seeing, that would otherwise be lost… well, we are past the limits of poetic emphasis, but there is no denying that this weave exists, of people and relationships, filling the gaps left by monopolies and terms of sale that would often blow fragile balances and household economies sky-high. A silent third sector, with no association and no wish to associate, but widespread and… an indispensable cushion.
