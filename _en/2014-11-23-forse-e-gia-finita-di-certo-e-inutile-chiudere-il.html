Perhaps it is already over. Certainly there is no point shutting the stable door: the horses have already bolted. Now there is a small myriad of people satisfied for a day, for a week, with a new right obtained, but on the happiness side the account always stays where it was. A few small satisfactions, nothing else.

Perhaps it is already over, ever since happiness stopped being the theme. Ever since there have been rights and no longer the taste of things, the meaning. The account, on the happiness side, always stays at zero, and the only alternative is to fill your belly with things, things, things. Whether they are satisfactions, food, experiences, money or rights, the account is always zero. Ferocious battles are fought, talking about anything at all except… except meaning and happiness. It is the civilisation of consumption, baby, and consumption consumes everything. Even the meaning of love, life, history, which first become words, then empty out, then… Ah yes, then they become rights. And so onward, all upright and righteous!

Let the crooked and the bent be silent for ever!
