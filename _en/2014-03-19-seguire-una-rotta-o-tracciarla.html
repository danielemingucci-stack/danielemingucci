We are in the middle of a crisis that is hitting the economy, society and the world’s balances with the same vehemence and the same violence as a war. Many observations can be made, but one incontrovertible underlying fact is that the models that have worked and brought results up to now are not, or will no longer be, effective. On the edge of this abyss, what effectiveness and what use can analyses have? There is the great psychoanalytic illusion according to which knowing the causes of the illness inexorably means being cured. It is not so. But even where it is not a matter of being cured but of growing, this does not seem the moment to rely on theoretical models developed from knowledge that, to a small or large extent, looks back and not forward. Even an analysis of the phenomenon and the development of social networks, today, speaks of a very recent past, but a past all the same.
Who will chart the road of the new?
The only possible protagonists are entrepreneurs, old and new, their intuition, their closeness to the market. To people, to ordinary folk, to real emotions and needs: brand reputation, social landscape already sound old…. Identification and risk: these must be the keys to action. It is the moment to chart courses, not to follow old ones. Following the market will not be enough - it will keep us alive only a moment longer - a new one has to be built.
No, it is the time of freedom and of new courses.
