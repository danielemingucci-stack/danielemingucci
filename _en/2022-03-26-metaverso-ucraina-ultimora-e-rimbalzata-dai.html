Breaking news. A report has bounced over from the US media according to which Meta is ready to host the refugees of the Ukrainian conflict in its own metaverse. According to leaks from a software developer who wishes to remain anonymous, giga-nurseries are being set up in which to host the refugees and connect them to a beta - but very advanced - version of Zuck’s metaverse. According to the source, the advantages brought into play are those of wiping out the refugees’ traumatic experience by taking them into a harmonious, comfortable setting able to let them recover and work through the drama they have just lived, and to carry on, practically straight away, a normalised experience of life. There are no details on the features of the giga-nurseries, although the imagination inevitably conjures up Matrix-style scenarios: it is precisely the possibility of virtualising these people’s life and experience, again according to the source, that is the strong point of Meta’s idea. Civilians, moreover, could even connect from bunkers located in the war zones and “skip” the traumatic event completely, reconnecting to the real world only once the conflict is over.

The news is certainly bound to cause a stir, for implications of various kinds, but an official announcement nonetheless seems imminent. A sort of “Life Is Beautiful” effect would be created, after Benigni’s film in which the protagonist, in some way, alienates his son’s consciousness, but where he does obtain the hoped-for result of keeping him from falling into despair. A borderline case, perhaps, but highly evocative of what a now very near future may become.
