Every day, for those who use a Kindle, Amazon offers a lightning deal: for 24 hours you have an ebook discounted by 75% on average. A real bargain, given that you buy for between 0.99 and 2.99 titles you would pay much more for. Above all at the beginning it was a real pleasure - I could not keep up with the reading - but now something has changed. They slap it down there, every morning, title author price… and a generic find out more. The thumbnail cover can barely be seen; of a review, of the number of pages, not even a trace. All information that can be found afterwards, to be sure, but they could at least put the genre…

It is a bit by the kilo, in short. The dematerialised book is already a trauma, one you can get over, but a trauma. But then sold like this, with just the price, it really risks being too much.
