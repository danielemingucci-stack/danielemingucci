Social media dishearten me a little because between chatter and substance it is very hard to tell the difference. Criticism and creativity (those who do and those who chatter, in short) stay two-dimensional and you cannot make out the depth. The background noise grows and… it is a bit like studying how to pass tests: tests are supposed to serve, I don’t know, to find out a person’s IQ, but by studying, one somehow falsifies the instrument. In the same way, by using social media well (I am innocent, I am on Tumblr!) you pump up your own profile, but you do not concentrate on the substance. Say you will do it, do it, say you have done it: the three rules of marketing my friend Bigi taught me… the real magic is inside that “do it”, is it not? How to do it, doing it well, living inside it, creating… not only emotions: things, relationships, research… The depth of this can be expressed, but it struggles not to resemble well-packaged storytelling which, unfortunately, only time teaches you to distrust… We have to try to live, in short, and I do not know whether social media help, but they certainly give no direction. I will try to rest a little, hoping to find other islands where Montemagno has not taught people to pitch camp (with all the affection I have for him, mind!) that light me up with no ulterior motives…
