Who knows how it went for those half-wits. They are not saying - like hell they are! - perhaps because there were a thousand of them and they are waiting for insinuations in order to launch an armed counter-attack, perhaps because there were only a handful and so… a handful of half-wits! They are certainly not straightforward, that they are not. Up there with Grillo and Bertinotti for tactics and vehemence, they look for a space and try to agree with one another for all they are worth, in answer to the unease of time passing and no, it is never the same again. Conserving, preserving, reacting, specifying, clarifying, underlining and wearing themselves out to keep everything intact without ever admitting that it is not enough, will not be enough, and that at bottom one feels bad, something is wrong… That, though, no, cannot be said; it is a sincerity that makes you weak, open to attack. The house of cards would collapse, and to keep everything standing one has to seek, at any cost, a form of unity, a concord, a unanimous judgement. And a precise one. Somebody has to do it, do they not? That everything be precise is fundamental: that it should not stray by so much as an amen, so as not to have anything to be forgiven for! Forgiveness is fundamental, heaven forbid, but it has to be generic, lofty, elsewhere; it cannot be contextual, just as friendship cannot be: the enemy of my enemy is my friend and… That sort of thing, unity is strength and then time will be the judge.
But today no, nothing is known yet: no video conferences are held and social networks are not used. One has to compromise oneself in person, damn it; somebody has to do it (this too) once and for all!
Certainly they will have felt a little of the warmth again, that of youth, being on the field once more, having an assembly in front of them. That atmosphere, in short; even if it had not come quite naturally, they would have worked to recreate it, within the limits of the possible, paying attention to the songs, to the details. As Giuss used to do, as Enzo used to do. And so on, each with their own points of reference. That is how it will have gone, or perhaps a little differently, but it is certain that the fate of Uzzah did not befall them, and that, damn it, must mean something! A road is marked out, a silent calling, a taking on of responsibility, a decision for existence. And that sort of thing, in short: somebody has to do it (again).
At least something has come out, I thought, but it did not satisfy me at all, quite the opposite. When unease takes shape it is sometimes positive, indeed it almost always is. But this time too the worst soul came out: that of judgement, the intellectual one, inhuman, far from the road, from one’s own road. The one that makes it impossible to stay close to one another, the one that is not sincere, but plans, arranges, organises and… judges, precisely. The worst of the worst, and detached, what is more, from any following, from any friendship. A scraped-together thing which, had there been five thousand of them, would disappoint me just the same, and I will not waste another minute on it. I am sorry, but one cannot even wish them a good journey. Still air, stagnant water stirring up the murk in a door-to-door that brings doubts, insinuations, perplexities… Stuff for old church hens, and less.
