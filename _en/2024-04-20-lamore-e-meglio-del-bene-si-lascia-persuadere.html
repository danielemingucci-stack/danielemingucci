It lets itself be persuaded, it changes, listens, looks, evolves. It generates. Taking it as a paradigm turns our way of looking at things towards openness and availability. Knowledge and visions are called into question and open up to new points of view, generating encounters, favouring connections, opening situations of dialogue. We do not start from a definition or from fixed parameters; we build fitting situations, case by case. The approach, the method, can evolve, affecting the parameters too, or setting/discovering new ones.

The approach of love generates virtuous behaviour, morality, and equality too takes on new features that keep it from degenerating into something to exploit in order to gain advantages and create imbalances, situations of favour. It is more complex, perhaps, but equally ordered.

Conversely, keeping to a morality does not certify love. Virtuous behaviour can be without vision, awareness, passion. It can simply start from an idea of good and manipulate everything in that direction: that is not love, but a form of re-education that may have no horizons adequate to the heart. To love you need a curiosity about destiny and about the future, not a predetermined idea of what that future must be, of its features. Loving, always projecting ourselves forward, makes us ready to be surprised, to create new scenarios, new situations. It is more than performing.

Loving becomes a project of change, and a service to create the conditions that make everything possible. Keeping alive the fertility of the land and of the heart. Performance comes afterwards, and changes its nature.
