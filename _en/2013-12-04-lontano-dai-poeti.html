I have never wanted to be near them, as has perhaps happened with artists or philosophers. Poets have never attracted me, that always affected broodiness. At least the Italian ones. Perhaps there is something sectarian, or just a sense of superiority, the impression one always gets that poetry is only for “them”, for those who can understand. That in other people’s lives there is little of it, or that it is not up to the level of the real kind, of theirs…
