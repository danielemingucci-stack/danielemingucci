It is what you see that gives you strength. The links that light up in your head and in your heart… what you see when you look, what you really see. There you find the strength for sacrifice, for toil, the enthusiasm to involve others, the wish to take one more step, the curiosity about what will come next… seeing. Seeing makes the difference. Seeing is living. If you do not see, you do not live. Not everything.
