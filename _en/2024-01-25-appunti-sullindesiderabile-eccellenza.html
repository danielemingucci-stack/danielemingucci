Anything worth doing is worth doing badly! Chesterton was always over the top, and he was surely mimicking the other Chester, -field, who praises the things that are all worth doing well.

Here, among us, everyone aspires to be an interpreter of Italian excellence. Almost a title to decorate oneself with, which I detest more and more. Shall we give it a perimeter, a character? It is something luxurious, well finished, with a slightly retro style and a high level of technology. Well made, but not in the German way. With more flair, a certain something that has to do with details and goes beyond “mere” function. And told like that, it is insanely cool…

But that word… “excellence”, his excellency, your lordship, eminence… it smells of old, of monarchy, of nobility… I do not like it, I do not like it, I do not like it. It does not tell of beauty, of care, of the passion that creates wonder, uniqueness, perfection, discovery and above all art. It tells of leadership, position, power, pre-eminence. It tells of caste, inner circle, élite. It does not tell of Italy; it tells of a small, residual piece of it, the decadent and snobbish one. And yes, associated with Italy on the international stage it even raises a smile. We have kept a few mini leaderships picked from among the scraps we were granted, we are arbiter elegantiae in a few situations, a buen retiro for the stressed rich, actors of a poor authenticity and of a not very responsible passion. Is it really worth thinking we can present ourselves as leaders? Does it not make more sense to set relationships in motion, to be Italians, inhabitants of an immense aircraft carrier in the middle of the Mediterranean, always protagonists of encounters, mixtures, contaminations and civilisations?

Excellence carries within it an ideal of conservation, something static, immovable. Decadent, in the past tense. Going forward is more beautiful, and whoever does so is not excellent, cannot be content with that, with making a name, with asserting a style. There: we have to stop at once leaving this as our horizon, aspiring to be or to become one of the many Italian excellences. It is time for the world, it is time for discovery, it is time for evolution and community… Identity.
