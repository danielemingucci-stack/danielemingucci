There are two ways of being creative (perhaps with mixes of the one and the other? Who knows). The first is to create magical syntheses, inventions or compositions, it does not matter: syntheses. The second is to add and add… This, with this as well and this, then this to do this and so on. Many ideas, usually too many, which do not force you to think much (and do not allow you to, in fact).
