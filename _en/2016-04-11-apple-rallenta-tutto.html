By now Steve Jobs would already have changed everything. There is the cloud, and it works. We hold in our hands objects with formidable, tiny processors. The networks are extremely fast. The time is ripe, and how, for having the “heart” in portable devices and the “body” in the old personal computers. Yes, like BlackBerry’s Blend. If Apple did it, properly, with all the apps working, the philosophy of the desktop computer at home, the stale mother (or rather the grandmother!) with her whole load of programs, RAM, ROM and all that jazz, would be forgotten in a few months. And instead? And instead they sell too many of them, and like hell will they stop! They keep us standing still, let time pass and enjoy the turnover. Steve Jobs was not like that; he bit the bone at once, the opportunity when it presented itself. All or nothing every time.
We could have an iPhone with all the access to data and the apps; you pair it with the Mac, any Mac and perhaps not only a Mac, the data and the apps run on the phone and… my home is the world. Full stop. Instead we have the iWatch, which is a small iPhone, which is a small iPhone Plus, which is a small iPad mini, which is a small iPad, which is a small iPad Pro and so on and so forth…
I miss him every day, Steve Jobs.
