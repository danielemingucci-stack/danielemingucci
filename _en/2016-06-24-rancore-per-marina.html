What rage, how much rancour. Someone dies who was barely born, in a tragic meeting of early and late that left so little to life… The time was all stolen; those who have
nothing lost even the little. No, it is unjust, but to think of how many stories we could have told so well instead of gathering incense to scatter over the heads. The heads and the elect, there to cultivate our expert doubts and make us delay and wait.
Before they went, themselves. And still promising, promising,
putting off those who could not tear themselves away, those who
innocently thought that only there was there any value, any
possibility…
How much rage, how much rancour, now that I am able to
imagine, and that I have seen and lived that one becomes a man
simply by walking forward and looking
around. So much for intensity! It is not born of staring, it arrives by walking. It finds us, not the other way round! Cultivating dreams and sincere aspirations, not uncertainties and imperfections.
Speed cleans our faces without our being able to notice,
while in the mirror everything always stays imperfect.
Those who stopped and waited got lost, mislaid their birth, their own.
How well we wrote, each with our own crookedness and
shortcomings; we were ready to grow by playing, but
they told us to wait, that there was time for putting ourselves
on the line and that one had to be ready. Ready like the elect, at the
opportune moment. And we did not play for a long
time. But how well Paola wrote, and strange
Marina too; why did I have to wait twenty years to find out?
Why? Did those who held us still then build something for everyone, or did they only go on fretting, afraid of competing and of losing places and privileges. Authority. To put yourself on the line you need a friend, to hope together and to hope for two. We
let ourselves be splintered and divided, we did not build among ourselves, or only by chance, so as to refer upwards and not horizontally. And we
lost so much.
