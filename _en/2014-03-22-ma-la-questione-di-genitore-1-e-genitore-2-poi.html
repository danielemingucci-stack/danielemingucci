But this business of parent 1 and parent 2, how does it work out when taken to the level of grandparents? I mean, among grandparents 1 to 4, who then decides the positions? In case of disputes or ill feeling, is some authority provided for? Not only that: if a pair of grandparents happen to be grandparent 1 and grandparent 2 of the grandchildren had by one child, will they have an acquired right over those had by other children too? Think of a child who calls his grandad grandparent 2, and then the cousin arrives and calls him grandparent 4… It could be a trauma.
I believe the matter should be regulated as soon as possible, and that every municipality should set up a register of parents from 1 to 2 and of grandparents from 1 to 4, to avoid any confusion and above all any possible attempt at false pretences.
I propose a petition.
