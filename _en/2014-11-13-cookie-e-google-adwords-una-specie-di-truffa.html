You visit a couple of times, say, a digital printing site or a car hire site, and the cookies go into action. Fantastic, in some ways: the internet understands what interests me and in future will steer me towards destinations better suited to my inclinations. In theory.
In theory, because in practice it so happens that that digital printing site and that car hire site are advertisers with both Google and Facebook on a pay-per-click contract: I browse merrily and find their banners and ads continually put in front of me… where are they taking me? Simply to a place where I have already been, and the two advertisers are paying Google and Facebook for a contact they have already had…
