Giving ourselves rules, it seems, means slowing growth down. That is the reasoning behind the quarrel over AI growth. Those who are losing control of the monster have taken fright, but in truth they have simply realised that growth cannot be only quantitative. The quality of growth also concerns what lies around it, the ecosystem, civilisation. A Wild West mentality, probably: I have rifles and ammunition, I am in charge. The rest will follow.

But it is not the only way to grow; there is another that creates order and harmony around itself, through principles and rules, through ideals and the building of meaning, of community. Seen this way, rules do not slow things down, they do not tie our hands; they help create civilisation. And quality. And growth. That is when you really win everything. It is not slowness, it is vision
