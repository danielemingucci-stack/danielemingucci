To compete at international level, a selection path for athletes is created. It is won by an athlete who then, however, loses in the international competition. Once, twice, three times… always the same athlete, always the same result: wins the selection and then loses. If in earlier years the same selection path produced athletes who instead won and won again at international level, the suspicion that when that particular athlete wins the selection, the selection itself is ineffective or even rigged is more than legitimate. All the more so if it is a team that changes over time and for which, therefore, one cannot speak of a psychological block, can one?
Now, Juventus: after ten league titles, twenty, thirty won in a row… you have to win the Champions League, and not once! Ten times! I was at the beach next to Costacurta: hey, how many Champions Leagues did that guy win?!?!
