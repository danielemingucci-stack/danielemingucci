There is no way to measure our individual values. We are free men and each of us is worth ten times what he earns. Twenty times.
We are a boat, and we measure the progress of the boat, not that of the sailors. We have no master and we are not masters: we are a crew, and together we steer the boat. We commit ourselves to this, each according to what he knows how to do, manages, wants, is able to do… Sharing among us the fruit and the fruits, looking one another in the eye often so as to be fair, as far as possible, in giving and receiving… We steer the boat; the boat is what we have and it is what we love, what we like having, what we like sailing. We, together, are the boat.
It is not the only way of doing business and it is not a way set against other ways of doing it, but it is our way, what makes us special, what will not change. For as long as we are free men.
