To those who tell you…

To those who tell you “I am not happy”, what do you answer? Am I? To those who tell you “I have had no luck”, to those who say “I no longer hope”, to those who say “I do not want to suffer”…? A road, a relationship, a commitment… how do you communicate something truly great, truly important?
