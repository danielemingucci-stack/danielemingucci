We’re five people sitting around a table and I have a small cake. If I eat it all by myself, it’s okay, it’s mine. Two of the four people with me are acquaintances, and one of them is a friend, but what does it matter? If I share it, I’ll lose half to four-fifths of my cake. I imagine they’ll understand.

That cake has a really good taste, almost telling a story and inspiring me. It’s exactly my idea of a good taste. And if I divide it into five pieces, this awareness multiplies five times.

Now, I’m in doubt. Do I really lose by giving away four slices and keeping only one for myself? What is the parameter? Ownership or community? Where is the true power?
