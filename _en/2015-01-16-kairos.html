“A limited freedom is not freedom”, “here there is the right to be ironic about everything”, any freedom at all regarding sexual orientation, the use of more or less narcotic substances, the beginning of life, the end of life… And whatever else.

It is a stringent logic, and a very attractive one too, the one that every time throws the ball forward towards more freedom, more freedom, more freedom. It is certainly a winning communication technique: those who want more freedom and more rights against the prudes who want limits, duties and prohibitions. Every time it is no contest, and the young at least are always on the same side.
Yesterday there were battles, today there are new ones, tomorrow there will be more: there is no ideological vision at the base of the cry “more freedom” that today demands freedom to offend religions, gay adoption, the right to euthanasia and so on. There is no project: there is an insatiable hunger, a bulimia for power, man’s challenge to reality. No: we do not want limits! And whoever fondly imagines that at a certain point they will stop is badly mistaken. They will never stop: more, more, more.
This is what the battles tend towards, because the edge of unhappiness that remains will always and certainly be the fault of some limit that has to be knocked down.

It is a hard war, very hard and without end.
No, not without end. Because limits exist: at the origins of our civilisation there is precisely the awareness of this limit. Our whole artistic, cultural and identity heritage was built on dialogue, on play almost, with this limit. Kairòs, a concept difficult to handle and to understand, has run through our whole culture and runs through it still, at least in its productive part… It is an affirmation and a negation together, a continual going beyond and a continual acceptance. An aspiration, full or empty depending on… oh well.

Limits exist, and against that limit we all stand together. Some fondly imagine that it is never enough, others fondly imagine they understand when it is enough, but the truth is told by Elsa, Wolinski’s daughter:


“Dad, it looks as if you are sleeping.
But you are not sleeping, you are dead.
For everyone else, Wolinski is still alive.
But for me, you are gone.
Elsa has lost her dad”.


Elsa, my heart bleeds with yours… you are the one who is right.
