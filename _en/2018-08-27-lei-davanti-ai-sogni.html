We get used, one day at a time, to seeing our dreams defeated or even humiliated. And it is not people’s malice, the cynicism of certain persons. It seems to be life itself, realism, that crushes everything into the possible, then into the probable, the usual […]. And seeing her smile open and light up everything when she tells how much energy, how much determination there has been in these years makes me proud […] truly strong in her impossible love. But I discover still more inside, because pride is not enough, not even love is enough to colour a smile so beautifully: I discover how much pain must have crossed her eyes every time she saw a dream humiliated, mocked or simply neglected. I discover that the sincerity of her heart, the purity of her gaze on things, has prevented me, one day at a time, from retreating even by a single step, has forced me to be simply a man. I discover that drowning in her eyes, sinking into the immensity her heart is capable of, is the road that makes me myself. The great woman is in front, not behind, because I - who am not a great man […] - I do not need support, I need inspiration, I need her eyes and the tireless thirst for beauty that rises from her heart. It is a mirror that makes the world beautiful, because it makes me a man in love with everything. […]
