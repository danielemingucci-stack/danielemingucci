I used to be afraid of the night; not any more. I lived the passing of the minutes with terror if I was not asleep, and every landscape, every room, every sleeping companion became horror. I filled the night with anything at all, with beer and cigarettes, with endless discussions, rides on the Vespa or in the car, kisses and caresses too. To escape those interminable minutes I cobbled together readings, writings like this one…
I did not know how to use my restlessness, I did not know peace, I was not sure of myself, of being. And perhaps I knew nothing about rest either, and entrusted it to sleep alone.
I have grown up and the restlessness is all still there, with me every night. But it is energy, so warm and happy that it often lets me rest. It has become rest, peace. It is directed at something, it is a companion, friendship (friendship, not a “friend”!)… It is me.
I do not sleep, every now and then, without fear. I have my heart beside hers, and not only hers. I have men and women beside me, I have life. I am rich in this poverty, I am on the way. I am inside a good embrace that whispers its name to me. I am challenged to meaning and to greatness. I am loved. In a fatherly way.
