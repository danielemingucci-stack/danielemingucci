«What does your dad do?». «My dad kicks aliens’ arses!». Can anyone afford less than this for their own child? Can we have a mission that is less than this? Can we live one day without being heroes? We are in the year 3000, and with all the resources we have at our disposal we cannot aspire to anything less.

If we are talking about enthusiasm, we are talking about something transmitted by osmosis, and Confucius is not enough: to slog every morning on the road or in the office, there is nothing you like enough. You have to know how to see something inside what you do. Which may even disgust you, but in life disgusting things too are done with enthusiasm when it is worth it (alias: when there is something inside). In practice I am a graphic designer, or a banking consultant, or a web specialist, or an engineer, designer, press officer, entrepreneur, promoter or consultant of something, whatever… but if the aliens invaded I would be one of the first out on the street, even with just a club!

Is this the enthusiasm I communicate? Do I realise how extraordinary what I have around me is, what I know how to do, what I have to do, what I am learning to do? Enthusiasm is born from the ability to see (not to look, to see!) something inside what you do. To search and to see.

If you do not see the aliens, how can you be a hero? How can you consider what you do important, how can you put into it the intensity needed to do it as well as possible? To see and to be moved, everything is born from there, a dear friend of mine used to say.
