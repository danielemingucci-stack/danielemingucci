L’amore può attendere (Love Can Wait)

What I would like to say when presenting it…

Written surfing on rage, on the sense of rebellion I feel when I see love dozing and crushed.

Love is something that happens in the gaze, something that lights up the heart, that takes place and transforms our awareness of everything around us.

It is not a feeling, it is not “something”, it is… a power, a capacity.

I rebel against the idea that it could be put after anything at all: there is nothing that deserves to come before love [fuck it, only love counts].

This is freedom: to love, to see, to be able to sense and experience the beyond; the rest is bullshit. The biggest bullshit of all is free will, because freedom is not choosing. Freedom is saying yes, discovering, being curious, flying. Free will is like choosing the optional extras on a car. Yes, fine, the coloured lights and the heated gear knob… but you buy the car because you have to go somewhere. You have to, you do not just go wherever you please: you need to move…

And I went into the hell that everything turns into when there is no horizon, as in Dante’s Inferno, where the real punishment is that the sky is missing, the stars are missing. It is an inhuman thing, we inevitably become monsters, and good-or-bad does not matter because it is no longer possible to be either good or bad.

Love is something that arrives from outside and unsettles everything, even time, even space. I believe it is so, that it can change even the past, that anything really can happen. So love cannot wait, that is a lie, it is bullshit. It is too urgent to look, to set ourselves in motion, to find our own strength, our own face in a “yes”. [Ours is the language of “sì”: thought, heart, mind are defined by yeses; I was born from a yes, I am loved and wanted by a yes, one after another… #digression].

To be sincere with love, to empty ourselves of optional extras and demands, to surrender, to dream, to begin to see. To suspend all judgement and discover the strength that is born from my yes, the unpredictable connection with meaning, with life itself, the living kind. With God’s dream… love is like that, and so to hell with bourgeois love, the good match, the soulmate and all the rest: those are calculations, they are judgements, they are tombs where there is neither air nor breath.

Love cannot wait, we must allow it to sweep us away now, now, without hesitation. To discover our next yes, and then the one after.

So: where am I ?
