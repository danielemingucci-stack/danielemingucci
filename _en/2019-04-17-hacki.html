Calling one’s own identity into question and looking for a channel of dialogue with those one sees every day, Saturdays included. The recipe is disruptive and well packaged. And it is precisely the packaging that deserves the most attention. Yes, because an innovator’s verve and a communicator’s manner certainly manage to convey a style, but perhaps they go to fill a “hole” that ought not to be filled. The idea of setting things in motion is always right, there is no arguing with that, but when the “material” is students the direction has to be chosen with care. Yes, because an education that is badly oriented, filled with methods and not with content, would objectively be an unforgivable betrayal of those who are building a vision of the world and of life. This is why the step back on one’s own identity really astonishes me: do you want to change your school? Good grief, it sounds like an invitation to leave. If you do not have an idea that is one more reason to come, you will be able to improve it… zero times “n” is still zero, is it not? And so on, with the great underlying question of why IT experts, robotics experts or videomakers should have more right than others to work on “ideas”. The stress seems to have shifted entirely onto the packaging, not onto the thinking.

Of course, innovating is also picking up on trends, being up to date, having tools for making things, but before all that one has to work on thought, on the quality of thought, on the ability to bring it to its best, to test it in the abstract and in the concrete. To explore the link between thought and language, to go deep in search of links, to develop the ability to recognise and catch them. To invent, and so to innovate… Everything that is normally done at school, in substance, in that magical dialectic between pupils and teachers, in that continuous relationship/comparison that makes it possible to throw seeds and see flowers bloom precisely starting from one “certain” given: the identity of the actors.

Are we sure that setting up the relationship by casting doubt on exactly this is a good start? And does staying too focused on methods really work? Let us try “re-reading” the texts of the promo to start measuring ourselves against “thought”: perhaps some logical link needs revising… no?
