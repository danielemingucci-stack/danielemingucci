We will find ourselves deciding on which platform to bring up our children, for example, taking sides with regard to the guarantees - shall we call them that? - or the cultural background, the censorship policies and so on. We will buy the box inside which to build our mentality. At first it will be a merge; low prices will allow a bit of this and a bit of that, but at a certain point we will have to decide: you cannot attend two universities (for time and cost), can you? The streaming “war” will lead there, and as users we will in the end make a choice of values: the productions will be equivalent, but the Disney TV version, say, will have a background of a certain kind, Netflix of another, and so on. It will hold at the national level too: Chinese, Japanese, Arab platforms… the differentiation begins.

Apple is not getting ahead of things, but it introduces a new element: a subscription included with the device. To have is to belong, but better still, to buy in order to gain access to content. Exclusivity, system… we will find out in time whether their content will stay only on Apple hardware (“we want to sell products”) or whether it will go onto other hardware too (“we want to shape mentality”, I do not think so). Boom. Off we go.
