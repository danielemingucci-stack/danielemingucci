I am possessed by dreams; I live on them because they become life in my days. They do not come true, they come true, but they always make themselves be chased, they keep the blood warm in my heart, they make it run fast in my veins. I am possessed and alive, I am armed, I am those dreams, I am those visions, ideas, intuitions. The energy of the days takes life from there, from their smile, from those that have come true, her, them, and from those still to be raised, hatched, launched, shared. I am alive with them; I have friends and stories to tell because of them… Am I not like the others, am I like nobody? How can one live without them, how can one die? Perhaps they ought to be ideals and not dreams, but no: ideals are inside dreams, they are inside action; when you move you affirm them, when you pull back or push forward they are true or they are only words. And friends, travelling companions, how can they stay alive and attached unless an aspiration is put on the line, the idea of a journey, a race… without putting aspirations to the test, where do we go? Towards what do we run, how do we find out who we are? I am possessed by dreams, I am full of friends, I am alive for them and, no… it is still not enough for me. I am sorry.
