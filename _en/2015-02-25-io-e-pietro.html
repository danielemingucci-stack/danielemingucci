I have a Saab 9-3 with two turbos, a sea of horsepower, sports suspension and blistering acceleration. He has a Multipla, a Fiat, on methane and with bald tyres, but every morning he has a go.

I see him in the mirrors lining up the cars, preparing the overtaking moves, making them and throwing himself into chasing me through the bends that wind their way up to San Patrignano. I do not remember him ever catching me, or even getting close, but every time I have had to keep an eye on him, work at it, choose lines and invent new overtakes. Above all make the “beast” count, always thinking that even with the cars swapped I would be the one in front.

No, that is certain. But it is a story that teaches something. Intensity, the goal, grit push the heart past every obstacle, past every objective lack, inferiority… A man is the determining factor: every means, every occasion comes after a beating heart.
With the vehicles swapped I will play my game… meanwhile do not lose my slipstream, my friend.
