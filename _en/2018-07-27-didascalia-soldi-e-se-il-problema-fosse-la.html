TITLE: Investing in creativity

If creativity is cultivated like a magic vegetable patch, everyone will go on hiding their own secrets like precious pearls. But those pearls are precious only if they become seeds, and they can grow only in a context that creates a system around them. An idea grows above all in the inner world of whoever invents it, certainly, but then it needs a thousand stimuli and comparisons to develop; above all it needs the widest possible context to express all its potential. Which is to say that creativity is something to invest in, but it is above all something to cultivate: if it is true that the first sparks are born in intimacy, it is truer still that only when put into circulation, only when forced into close comparison, can they light the fire they are capable of. The tendency of so-called creatives, instead, is to shut themselves away in secret (terrified, most of the time) to give birth to new inventions, reserving joy for the few moments of celebrity in which to present themselves as phenomena, not quite freak-show ones but almost, to be praised and admired, in an unnerving existential chiaroscuro that usually goes out in slow melancholy… We can do better, but we have to put ourselves on the line. Everyone, a little or a lot, is creative: it is hard to believe, but it is so.
