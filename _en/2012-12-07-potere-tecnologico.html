There is a secondary obsession with power: that of technological power. No: of the technological “it can be done”. It is the one that leads to the building or inventing of extremely complicated and unusable products. One adds and adds and adds, with no identification with the possibility of finding one’s way in the maze being created. Or research is developed towards something that indeed “can” be done (or rather: can also be done), but that leads far from the sense and from the intuition that had generated the process. Intuitions that chase and follow one another in a delirium of technological power. There are thousands of extremely complicated or senseless apps to bear witness to it, produced by those convinced that the game is played on “functions”.
