Love has nothing to do with life. It is an accident, a blow, a crash. It is not a feeling; feeling is the thing that has least to do with it. And life is its food.
Love has no object that is enough for it, a woman a body a world to conquer. Love is theft, blind violence against everything that is yours, against everything you want.
Love is only for heroes; the others deserve much less and cannot even imagine its effect.
Their feelings, the things they feel drawn to, the introverted search for what really and a l l t h e w a y d o w n corresponds… what are we talking about? Foot massages?
No, it is all elsewhere. It is death, but not the comfortable death of a glorious instant: it is an energy that runs through the muscles and the gaze. It is an energy that makes every gesture sacred, with fire. Love is not for kids, neither for the beautiful nor for the ugly. Love is for those who want to be a man, and a man is sure of one thing only: he has to die for something.
What sense does all this make?
