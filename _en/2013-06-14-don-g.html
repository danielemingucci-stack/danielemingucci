I was at the back, to one side…

It was a mass in the middle of a week of the usual urgent work, after all.

I was at the back, things were going along.

How else would they go, after all…

With great intensity, with so much care and love, even, a funeral is a funeral and things are what they are.

I think there were no pews, everyone standing so as to have more room and all be there. But I do not remember much else (how beautiful the Duomo is). Then it ended and you turn to leave, looking for a few glances to greet, without enthusiasm, politely. Many measure their grief, and perhaps one ought to behave accordingly…

I turn to leave, I do not take even a step, the guitar and then those voices, familiar to my heart… “Al final, del suo viaggio nella vita resterà…” and my heart begins to gush; he arrives impetuous, as he always arrives, the Don. Brazen and ignorant, as we say round here. There, that is his legacy. Nothing has ended.

“Restiamo quelli che possono sorridere, sull'orlo della morte, in piena luce”.

In full light. Happy birthday, Don.
