There is a way of looking at people and friends that is always suspicious. Not distrustful, but suspicious. Doubtful, that is it. The doubt is simple: “will they be the right ones? Will they be tough enough types? Will they be really capable, or am I wasting time? Will they not lead me into some blind alley?”. And so on… Living like this, every step is a verification, a check. A doubt, precisely. And one is always in need of reassurances and confirmations. In the last analysis one is alone… because all the way down one cannot trust anybody. I repeat, not in a malevolent sense, but in the sense that nobody is enough.
Alone, in the wrong place. At bottom it is a doubt about one’s own destiny…

There is another way, trusting and open, curious to see what destiny will invent, what my friend will invent, what will become of me, where I will end up, how it will turn out… And it is not a letting things be, taking whatever comes, because it is a being there full of questions and issues, full of choices, of things and people preferred to others. Certainly, though, it is a way of always being in the right place, with the right people, before great challenges. It is a way of referring any present to destiny, and so it is never feeling alone or wrong… Or small.

What is the secret?
