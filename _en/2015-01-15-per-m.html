I am a male and I understand nothing. I have always tried to die quickly, I have, because I like finding out what is at the bottom. Now I am more afraid, but only because of the lost look of my children and of… of her, who lights up my heart and my eyes. I have never been ill; I do not know whether I bear things well or whether I have never had a real pain. But I am always unwell. I have limped for twenty years, I sleep badly, I adore sunsets. And dawns. I cough, even though I no longer smoke. A strange smell is enough and I retch. Even when I brush my teeth, every morning. My stomach is weak, and my gut.
Since I got old I am always afraid, but quite the opposite too. I would not go back even a day, not even an hour. Start again? I would not dream of it. The aches, the wounds, the scars? All medals, all pride. And courage: you can add a few to me, but you cannot take any away…

But the infinite wonder of an instant… that I understand. When you see all things perfect, spellbound in the tenderness of their destiny, form becoming road… It is true: the road is beautiful, but only if it ends, I think. It is beautiful along the way too, like the sea, but it is made for carrying, it is a cradle. A rough one.
On the road we roll, we never really feel it, because it wears us away. It carries us off, but it also carries off infinite parts of us little by little. It is wicked, the road, if you want to live. If you want to live, the road will not do. Stay away from the road, it carries you off, it carries something off every instant. And it fills you with things that are not yours. It takes room from the most precious memories because it adds adds adds.
That is strange too, it carries off but it adds…
Stay away from the road, there! It changes you, the road! It takes things from you and swaps them for other things…

But I am a male and I understand nothing. I like the road. I accelerate so that it ends sooner.

It is madness, but I am certain. I am going home.
