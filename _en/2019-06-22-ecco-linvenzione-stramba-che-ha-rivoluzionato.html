The genius is Mister Hyde

I know Daniele Mingucci; he is not a genius. He is a depressive who struggles to control his anger. The episodes one could cite - from rags to… rags - are countless. This very evening he flew off the handle to the point of trembling over the untidy bathroom and a risky overtaking manoeuvre on the motorway. His thoughts of revenge pushed out towards scenarios from the Thousand and One Nights, Stephen King and Kafka. No, Freud… But the most forbidding enemies are those of the past, the memories of when, young and unsure, he could not find a road, a dimension. And respect, or rather the regard of others. Then life is kind, and perhaps offers prizes and second chances, but not even those are enough to quiet all the anger, the rancour, the stifled cry of a reality which, in all its wonder, is never enough. And everything gets mixed, a confused blend of beauty rancour fullness wonder mistakes mistakes mistakes, inconsistencies, something undefined, full love, the inability to fly - because flying is the necesse, never mind sailing - together with the various messes. And one throws oneself into dazzling thoughts, with energy, so as not to give in to the infinite abyss that swallowed Mister Hyde until it ate, literally, the other, the established scientist. An exasperated challenge to the abyss gurgling in the heart, an exhausted resistance that broadens its shoulders swimming against the current of the whirlpool and, merely in order not to drown where everyone drowns - with small or great aspirations - surpasses itself.

It is not genius, it is not positivity, it is struggle, it is the inability to accept, even the most obvious limit, one’s own. It is searching, rebellion against the greyest, blackest ogre… He does not want to be left alone imagining beauty, he needs everything to become real and the damned prophets of doom, who know how little his heart is worth, they do, to be silenced by a definitive word. Possible, true… incredible.

The anger overflows every embankment, it kills, perhaps only in the mind, it tortures, knocks down, grinds minces freezes, then it subsides and weeps for itself. Anger is the whirlpool, fear is the whirlpool, ineptitude, despondency, life, everything… Daniele is not enough for himself and looks for an outlet, he dreams, imagines, moves his hands, screams weeps vomits. He loses control of himself, and only thank God does he not have enough energy to destroy everything, shitty Thanos. So he surrendered to that hateful limit and duelled with the horror, Conrad’s and Spielberg’s, that of the children of the seventies, of the bomb and The Day After… until he learned that only a smile can push the tears back and make them shine, like gleaming bombs, to become dreams, strength, embraces, a world of friends.

Daniele Mingucci is only a depressive who resists the horror, the fear, the filth he has inside, who does not know how to smile enough at his own children and who was saved by an angel.

I write this out of a sense of responsibility, because there is nobody less gifted than he is, nobody more unsuited to life, to invention, to courage. Only that he, like a stupid bumblebee, does not know it… and he flies. Fly higher than him. You deserve it, the world deserves it, Greta Van Fleet and your children.

Thank you, Michele,

a friend.
