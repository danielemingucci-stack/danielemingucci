There are right ideas and wrong ideas. Then there are false ideas, which are the ideas of the false, of those with an interest, of those willing - and often also skilled - to lie. They are ready-to-use ideas, simple for the most part, and always fit to slot in. Lego ideas, with no identity until they become part of a form. That is, of an interest. The end becomes the means, everything gets messed up, and the turds - not even all that magically - float as always.

[Dedicated to second-rate poets 😂]
