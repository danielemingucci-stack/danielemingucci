I had set off with a suitcase without dreams and a car loaded with old ones. Emptied, suddenly, of everything: after so much striving I had nothing in my hands. I loaded every single thing and left the most beautiful city because it no longer had a centre, and in a few months it had devoured everything, all its own beauty and something - a lot - of my life. Had it ever really been beautiful? I doubted even that, emptied as I was.

And I ran towards home, breaking off the journey for a folly, a minuscule, very thin possibility. I abandoned a great ship for a small boat, a lying poet told me, but I took command and headed south towards “my” sea… It was the first of April, so I could have said I had been joking, but I never went back, and I turned my gaze only for quick memories. I put to sea, I found some of my dreams again, I began to build new ones, I prepared the ground.

I set off empty, full of things that were no longer any use, and I began the journey. I gave credit to hope, I made it untameable, and I destroyed the reasonable, obvious walls that were stealing my gaze. And my air.

Yes, it was a gesture I could have not made, put off one day at a time, with good reason. Commitments, other possibilities, duties and promises: everything could have held me back without my being able, as in a book by Conrad, to find the sea… A breath of wind guided me, of sincerity. The only resource capable of freeing the heart and the life that is inside it. The whole mystery of infinite possibilities began to untangle only with a gesture of sincerity before my own heart.

Twenty years ago, with Marco and Sapo, Empresa Creativa was born…
