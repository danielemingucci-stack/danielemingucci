There is substance, but there is also form. The road and the walking, destiny and life. They are concepts that stand in a permanent relation to one another, and of which, in the end, it is really hard to grasp the priority, the preponderance… One without the other is emptied out, in fact. A shit film with a great ending, or the opposite, is not what we desire: something inside us always cries out for a harmony, for having it both ways, on which life may weaken you, but… we cannot desire less.

That is why, when they sugar the pill for you on any subject, when they decide for you and leave you out, when falling into line is the only option, even if you are being led to the truest of truths, to the best of options or opportunities, well, something grates. So loudly, sometimes, that you want to throw the baby out with the bathwater, to break the bank and send everything flying. I, every I, wants to be there, not to decide, but simply to live. There is a need to take the steps, not to skip the bends, to be protagonists, to arrive together with the others. You cannot hold your nose and live only the ending, an ending decided by others…

On this level, yes, what matters is taking part.
