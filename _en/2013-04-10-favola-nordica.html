This is the glorious story of Vinsent, born of Mansen son of Munsen, king of Tubinsen in the land of Olfers.
Vinsen, together with the faithful Ansen, wizard of Vulsen, went to the court of Princess Milzen. Along the road, though, they met Falsen, enemy of Munsen and of his son Mansen, king of Tubinsen in the land of Olfers.
The perfidious Falsen had with him the furious dragon of Balsen: Ponzio.
Ponzio, dragon of Balsen, had long loved Princess Milzen, daughter of Mansell, brother-in-law of Falsen. They too were going to her court to ask for her hand or to abduct her on the sixth, the sixth of December, when it is colder than ever.
The heroic Vinsent at once took aside Ansen the wizard of Vulsen, and said: “Quick, let us run, and call Pilsen too, Mansell’s owl! Let us go to the castle of Prince Karsen, brother of Milzen and father of Malzen, the child princess.
The wizard Ansen, forthwith, turned himself into a very dense wood where Vinsent could hide together with Pilsen. At a run they took the steep path that led to the hill of Karsen’s castle, and there, as soon as they were beneath the walls, they cried: "A dragon, quick, is after us! Help, help, open the manor so that we may forthwith be saved! Help, help”. At once the drawbridge fell across the moat that surrounds the castle, and those two and the wizard of Vulsen went in quick as quick.
Prince Karsen, informed by his men, hurried to receive them in the royal hall which, in a time gone by, had been the scene of dances, of feasts and luxuriant banquets.
Listening, he listened, Prince Karsen, to the breathless tale of the three fugitives, and meanwhile, absorbed, he thought and thought how to save from the bestial fury of the animal dragon his dear sister, his beautiful princess whom he so esteemed. “I love her!” cried all of a sudden the proud Vinsent “and it will not be that stupid stinking dragon that keeps me from her! Give me a sword, I am going out to cut his nails, and if need be his crest too and something more!”.
“You cannot go out alone and run to meet him” exclaimed Prince Karsen, worried.
“Oh no? We shall see! Give me sword and armour, quick”.
Full of admiration, Prince Karsen ordered his servants to prepare Vinsent the valiant for battle. The wizard Ansen cast a good spell, and the owl Pilsen gave the prince a feather for his crest.
