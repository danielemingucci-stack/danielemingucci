A perfect scapegoat, Balo is bringing out the best of Italy’s home-grown frustration (that is what football is for, is it not?).
Always with his headphones on, rich, arrogant, spoilt, good-looking, indolent, talented, explosive, black… Super, above the average. And people, as always - as with Valentino, as with Tomba and so on - are there waiting, waiting, waiting…

Well, the World Cup is the perfect occasion: this one is not only black, he scores only one goal… Let us massacre him. And who gives us the cue? Well, it is clear: the real men, Buffone and De Rossi, the ones who in 2010 in South Africa, do you remember them with wobbly legs? Not only that: the good men too, Prandelli who has the code of ethics (except for the Juve players, because you never know) and who did not even need to ask for it, the million-euro salary…

No, I would say we are not there. Every chain is as strong as its weakest link, but here the weak link is not Balotelli.
Here the weak link is the frustrated rage of the mediocre against talent.
Here the weak link is the average Italian who in the end always trusts the senators (for life). But the senator (for life) is precisely the one who lives on a right of position, on a pedestal, who says how things must be and never calls himself into question.
And then the good guys. Well, the good guys make the rules, which for them are special rules, and then they live off the income, the gilded income of their goodness.

Value, performance, is not created this way. Value is created by channelling anger towards something positive. It is not created by putting up with things; it is created by taking risks, by creating the conditions so that “the difficult ones” do not remain “useless”… The inverted commas because it is Vasco’s stuff: we are free to get it wrong, and luckily free to come back.
Let us salute with respect the senators, and the good guys. May they enjoy their gilded pensions.
Balo, Cassano and all the difficult ones, let us hope they come back as soon as possible and that even the mediocre and the frustrated who frequent the stands can enjoy their talent. The most beautiful thing.
