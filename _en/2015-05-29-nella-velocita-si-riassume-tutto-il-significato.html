In speed is summed up the whole meaning of our civilisation. The great craving of the western soul, its strength, the true secret of its every progress, is expressed in two words: “more quickly!“ Our life is driven by this violent desire, by this painful insatiability, by this sublime obsession: "more quickly!

Luigi Barzini, Peking to Paris
