The January Wired arrives (on the 16th, but by now I am in no hurry) and I notice a small change: “stories ideas and people that change the world for the better”. Why “for the better”? What need was there?!? Then, either you put the comma between “stories” and “ideas” or you take out the “and” between “ideas” and “people” (it is called asyndeton, but either you do it or you do not).
I turn the cover, an Audi R8 advert: in Italian it reads “ROtto”, broken, do not emphasise it! And instead: Rrrrrrrrrrrr8… It almost makes you think of a burp.

Antonelli writing as a youngster I am skipping altogether for today: there is no getting through it and I could not bear it… Yes, I know, perhaps always asking for the Moon is wrong, but was there not something halfway before crashing to the ground?
