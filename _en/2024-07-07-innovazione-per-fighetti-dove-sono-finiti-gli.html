Where have NFTs gone? And the blockchain? AI has wiped everything out and is drawing the whole stash towards itself. A stash of chatter, for those far from the control rooms. Today the match is opened by Fattor Comune, with a podcast of knowing little smiles against the teachers who want AI out of school. Traitors to the evolutionary process. Put like that, good heavens, it sounds like big stuff, gravely guilty towards the cognitive development of these generations. Oh yes, understandably. Teachers are irritating in the way they handle their quarter of an hour of power, when they decide who lives and who dies, separating winners from losers and so on and so forth. But that is an anger that ought to pass after a certain age: it has to be managed, put in its place. What is the point of studying Latin, Greek, mathematics… To give history the billionth translation of the same passage, the trillionth calculation got right or wrong? The point of school is having been there. It serves to learn to measure yourself against others, it serves to get angry and to put yourself on the line, to relate. To enter a system, with rules, with a logic that could well be different and so on and so forth, but which has a function of e-ducation. It is father and mother, it sets embankments, it brings out value, school does. And it makes you suffer, rage, despair, hate. And then love, thrill, live. Which is to say that evolution is a process of relation, between the present, the future and the past. The new, which is built against and beyond tradition, on top of tradition. And the present, we, I, you now, are this living connection. You evolve by accepting a relation with what you have around you, with what came before, with what may come after. It is not doing your homework well; it is learning, growing, forging yourself. Discovering, inventing, devising and so on.

So the hurry to send packing the obsolete and backward guardians of a dusty past could perhaps be called into question. They are too important a player; they are the lines of the playing field, the ones that allow talent and technique to evolve and express themselves. Yes, because evolution has a sense. A direction, yes, but also something more. The subject of this evolution is the only interesting sense. The people, the individuals who know how to become protagonists of this process.

Now, having a few AI addicts at school firing at you all the little tricks and all the wonders of a tool that helps you do your homework faster: does that really help? Is it a process of evolution? Does it not resemble all those little Google soldiers who come into your company to teach you a marketing that in the end is always “buy a service from Google or Meta”? School, study, the desire for knowledge, life, love, culture, politics, work: certain things are hooked to fundamentals that, yes, can find new tools and new efficiencies, but cannot avoid remaining human. If they are not hooked to the human they are of no use, not in the sense that they do not work, because they work brilliantly, but they have no link with anything interesting. Is AI really intelligence? Old Noam says no…

Now my questions: do we want to be protagonists of innovation, or to ride whatever comes along, year after year? Is ours, in short, innovation for posers with a Tesla, or will we sooner or later set some processes in motion? Yes, we have to decide who we want to be…

I close with an aside: Italian secondary schools are universally recognised as the best in the world. Crazy, eh?
