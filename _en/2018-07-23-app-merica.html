Today’s myth, for some years now, is to hit the jackpot by creating an app. And the first part of the myth is the illusion that the present system rewards ideas and those capable of having them. It is not so. The other pious illusion is that it is a free system, in which everyone has the same weight and the same chances. The net, instead, is American. Android is American, iOS is American, Facebook is American. And so on. In America, moreover, I believe the law protects the intellectual property of software; in Europe we have nothing similar. The battlefield, the enclosure in which we run about, has an owner, and we play by his rules. They are comfortable rules, it is true, under which talent is very often rewarded, but the whole mythology of the startup born in a garage has above all the great merit of making us throw ideas for free into the net, of creating an almost infinite catalogue of intuitions, solutions, ideas precisely, from which those with means and control have only to choose. Instagram was the same as dozens of other apps (Italian ones too), but it was “chosen”, bought, integrated. The others disappeared. It is an example. All of us throw in ideas upon ideas, optimisations, models… the market rewards them, gives them value, makes them grow, then beyond a certain size you do not go: the happy ending is being bought (which is a great thing, to be sure), so control of the system always stays in the same place. The norm is that sooner or later something similar and a bit better pops up, perfectly integrated with the giants with whom it is not even possible for us to begin a dialogue.

Enough. I have already said it twice. I repeated it only to say clearly that if value is created on that side, it is created, for free, for others. If you want to do something for yourself, you need to aim at something else, to create models that generate value outside the net. Yes, it sounds like stellar nonsense, but I am talking about models in which the net is “a” means, not the key value. Networks of people, distribution networks, the circulation of ideas. Among all the digital media, the web is the one that is relatively most free: iOS, for example, can throw you out from one hour to the next with no reasons whatsoever. We have to think outside the airless ecosystems they are forcing us into in this period, fortunately with relative success because of the extremely high speed at which everything transforms. But even if these guidelines were wrong (I for example think that the weak side of the American model is, paradoxically, the algorithms: they do not know how to trust people, so much do they love control), the mythology that has been created - I am thinking of gurus like Montemagno, Riccardo Luna (I adore them both) - is certainly in fact servant to a master, knowingly or not. How much is the space worth that Google gives away every day to Wikipedia’s first position? Is that fantastic encyclopaedia really so free? And all the space we ourselves give away to “follow us on Facebook” and so on, in media spaces that we buy, pay for, fill… We are all giving away a lot, a great deal, forgetting an easy lesson, that the medium is the message, that is, that the fruit of all our creative effort will remain almost entirely with others, with the manager of the amusement arcade. The adventure is elsewhere; our creativity deserves more room.

Do not say to me any more “I have thought of an app that…”. I love the sea and infinite spaces. And the truly ironic thing is to publish these lines… on social media.
