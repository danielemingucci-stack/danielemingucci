Oh yes, on the pitch. There they always prove everything. In their own way, but everything is always proved. We will never know why, under Luis Enrique, Totti had to be rested precisely against Juve (which needed those points, how it needed them), or why under Zeman they are now going in slow motion.
I will never know, because a football which in 2006 discovered that we had all been made fools of for three years is one I no longer watch. And before? And after?
But it is the cowardly arrogance of that “on the pitch” that disgusts me most of all. Those people who shove “the results” in your face, the proofs “on the pitch”. The very ones who stole everything, gave back almost nothing and started again as before: they shove the results in your face! Well!
The results are exactly the filth we see, the filth of “anything goes”, the filth of the ugliness people pretend not to see, because the results speak. But at bottom it is right like this: the team of Fiat, which with public money, the money of all of us, has taken Italian design where it has taken it, which keeps the best things in its belly and feeds us the leftovers, which wages war on the Germans without having a saloon in the D segment (just to say what it means to have a superior vision of reality)… Well, this is the team of seven million Italians. It ought to be the national team! Juve the national team! And long live Italy.
No, I no longer watch football, and it is not that I feel defeated. What disgusts me is the army of frustrated people who enjoy victories not their own and enjoy taking it where they take it, even thinking they are protagonists of anything at all …
