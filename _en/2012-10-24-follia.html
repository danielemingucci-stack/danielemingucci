Madness brushes past me every day, the last bulwark of hearts that do not surrender. Hearts of friends, defeated perhaps, who will not lose. Gnawed by a woodworm of unreasonable hope - mad! - unable to do sums, unable to surrender. Unable, perhaps, certainly, to realise, but stronger even than reality. An opaque madness, never lucid, a depressed, bitter madness. Pervaded at bottom by a flash, a flash of inexorable genius, cowardly before real life, but untameable. Buds of imperfect flowers that will sprout, perhaps, only in paradise. Buds not to be missed by those who want to enjoy it straight away (paradise).

What will my friend come up with tomorrow? What will destiny come up with?
