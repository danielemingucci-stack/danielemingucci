There is a window that looks out opposite my house, with trees damned by diseases. Old chicken thieves gather their fruits: parasitic mushrooms grown on dog droppings. Trees always detested, capable of losing leaves in every season of the year, probably immortal and no good even for giving shade when it is needed.
There is a window facing out opposite my house, black with these rancours.
