Social networks are not the future, they are the present, and does anyone know what comes after? Because in future we might get fed up. It will be bots and artificial intelligence, together capable of anticipating our desires. And after? When we are at the level of total satisfaction, what comes after?

In a few years of Facebook I have satisfied almost every curiosity: I have found almost everyone again, I have followed and seen again, I have seen that video - yes, I have seen it (can you get through a dinner or an evening without someone asking?) - and I have tried living in worlds far from my own, immersed and carried away. I got fed up. Is Twitter better, or here, Tumblr (the coolest of all, the safest place for a secret :), Instagram? LinkedIn, Snapchat and the new WhatsApp? Everything is better than Facebook but nothing is different. No.

Other fashions, other satisfactions, other curiosities to fill, then the daily grind. The next step (BOT + AI) will fill new spaces, but will it manage to be different? No, because I am in a hurry, and social media, today, are starting to slow me down after having led me on (like this damned touchscreen, after the splendid keyboards of my BlackBerrys!). I am in a hurry to go and to build; messing about and consuming will not be enough for me. Contents, experience, reality. Life.
