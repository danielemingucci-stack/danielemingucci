You no longer know how to dream, my friend
On your page there are only numbers
That do not know how to fly.
Drawings, sometimes,
But only to remember that you can
And you write numbers all around
To prop them firmly to your pages
And to your sums.
The words that make us fly
The ones that make us dream
No longer measure your steps
You drag your feet counting the stones
Of the road, thinking that sweat
Gives them some value.
But we do not build the road
We inhabit it, we travel it.
It is our home and we sleep every night
Under a different sky, with stars that shine
Above us and above our dreams.
Stars are not counted, my friend
Because they are not ours and they are there
Only to make our eyes shine
When they look up
Before flying.
Lift your gaze from those tired pages
Waste a little time on flying
And do not measure a life
That you will have to give back. My friend.
