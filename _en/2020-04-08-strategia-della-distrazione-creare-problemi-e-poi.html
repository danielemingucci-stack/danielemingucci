create problems and then offer the solutions (everyone – not me – is already waiting, quivering, for the fabled vaccine);

strategy of growing gradualness and of the sudden surge (limitations that are gradual and then ever tighter);

strategy of deferring (presenting a solution as “painful and inevitable”);

use the emotional side more than argument (apocalyptic images, war bulletins);

keep the public in ignorance and mediocrity (the virologist cannot be questioned, we are not capable);

encourage citizens to be complacent with mediocrity (flash mobs and other mass demonstrations);

Address citizens as if they were children (the words of the Governor of Lombardy: “If you do not understand it the nice way, tomorrow we will make you understand it the hard way”);

insinuate a sense of guilt (we are all potential contaminators and plague-spreaders, we are all guilty, we are set against one another by this shameful laying of blame);

know individuals better than they know themselves
