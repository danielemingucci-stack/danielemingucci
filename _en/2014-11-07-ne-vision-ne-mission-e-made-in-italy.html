Vision and mission are always confused in Italy. Those who have studied in the specific field are probably anything but uncertain on the matter - they might be alarmed, if anything - but if you start talking about it point-blank, I think eight entrepreneurs out of ten may be uncertain, to say the least. In Italy…

Well, perhaps this is exactly the secret of Made in Italy, this “lived” coincidence, this not having clear in practice what the difference is. Considering them “practically” the same thing in a certain sense brings the vision into the present, as if we wanted to possess it straight away, straight away as experience, straight away as taste… Something is lost in the complexity of projects; a great deal is gained in the ability to tackle the immediate complexity of the process, in the care for detail, in fitting it into an overall harmony that is always… how shall I put it… present.
