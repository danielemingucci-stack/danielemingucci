I would rather not pray for that as well, to be well, to have no troubles. For money, above all, for that I would rather not even hope. I would like to be elsewhere, to fly with thoughts and with actions, to plan dream meet. To challenge reality only in the positive, straining only towards the upper limits, putting aspirations on the line without minding the petty, stingy sums of the crafty, the sly and the tight-fisted who, one penny at a time, one signature at a time, take everything away from you while you are facing elsewhere.
I do not want to discover that the pessimists were right, those who have never risked anything and above all those who have never dreamed and no longer know how. I do not want to believe that hope is empty, that one should not always believe or, more still, that it is better never to believe.
I do not want to think that there are the predestined, the right ones and the wrong ones, and that talent, at least one talent, one chance, everyone has, and has every day.
