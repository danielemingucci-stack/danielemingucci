Do not form partnerships to develop a project. It is the best way to weaken it, if not to undermine it at the root. At the very least everything slows down, but almost certainly the partners’ goals are different and the cracks widen rather than close. Better clear commercial agreements, distinct roles, professional supply. Partnerships do not save money, do not improve quality, do not enrich the project.
