The sea draws the sand,

it does so with the help of the wind.

It is a story of waves and gusts

that carves the instants for ever.

Stones, shells, debris

lend themselves to an unplanned journey:

they roll, hesitate, set off,

listen to every sound.

All around, the sand caresses things,

with the wind and the water it carves

soothes rounds.

The sea draws, surrounds,

embraces.

The wind moves it,

riles it, ruffles it,

then runs off and lets it settle.

Lets it shine

(with sun and with moon)

with its blue copied from the sky,

lets it rock

the days and all the nights as well.

Then it starts again,

comes back and goes again

(every day)

a little this way, a little that.
