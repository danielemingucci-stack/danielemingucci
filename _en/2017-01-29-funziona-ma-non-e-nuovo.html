I am in bed, my wife is already asleep and I am reading a little. On the tablet, where inevitably, as you browse, you arrive at the video feature. It is late, there is silence, nothing doing: I do not watch it. I could put my headphones on, but then it is too short a step to finding myself completely alienated, wearing a headset and writhing in bed :).
No, with video we are really not there. That it is effective and engaging, good grief, there is no doubt, but that the future lies there is, at the very least, a badly formulated concept. If the future is there, then it means we are “still there” after fifty years. No, because videos leave you in a disheartening passivity, they are very expensive and take a very long time to produce (let us not joke, eh? To make decent ones the thousands of euros come raining down), but above all they are slow, extremely slow to take in. Three minutes, two minutes, five minutes… devoted to introduction, development, conclusion where, with two drawings, five seconds might have been enough.
To be new it needs to be faster, to improve the performance of perception, to speed it up, to complete it… videos are good for sweet-talking, for sugaring the pill, for entertaining, damn it! It works, yes, but nothing new…
