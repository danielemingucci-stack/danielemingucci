Dear Barzini,
So there are those who say that after all: – after our labours of two months, after the rocks and the rivers, the sands and the forests, the mud and the banquets through which we passed – there are those who say that our journey has shown one thing only, namely: that you cannot go by motor car from Peking to Paris.

Scipione Borghese, Isola del Garda – September 1907
