It will be only an instant
just one
when you see his smile again.
Everything will be embraced
and love
at last
infinite.
Your pain will vanish and the tears
will be full of joy.
Nothing will have been lost
in the wonder
of your embrace.
You will caress his face
and hold him again
glorious
and great.
It will be only an instant.
Do not be afraid.
