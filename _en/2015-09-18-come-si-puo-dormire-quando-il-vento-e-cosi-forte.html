How can you sleep
when the wind is so strong?
You try with the television
to anaesthetise the senses
but every gust sounds
like an alarm clock for the heart.
The wind runs and you are indoors,
without a sail to cling
to its speed.
What will the direction be today?
Where will it carry those who have sails to follow it?
A strange rage is born,
the rage of a flight that does not come
yet.
The house seems to swell and burst,
outside everything is upside down, flown, flung about
in the hysterical whirls between trees and houses.
Bring me the wind, life, let it carry me.
I am looking for my harbour, I am looking for my sea.
I am ready.
Please.
