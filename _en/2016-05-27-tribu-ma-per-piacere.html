The tribe is an explicit image, with no metaphor. A nightmare place from which we have freed ourselves, almost, through centuries of struggle and suffering. A place where balances of force and mutual control hold sway, dominant bloodlines that keep hegemonies generation after generation, fixed balances and never the chance to shuffle the cards, to redistribute tasks and opportunities. The thing furthest from the valuing of merit and from the possibility of generating movement. Utterly disgusting. A crap idea, Rondoni…
