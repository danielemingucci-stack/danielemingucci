Vote-trading has entered politics endemically, and it is intimately inherent in the very concept of support, of a declared vote. No politician would ever accept support given “once in office”; it would be too late, it would make no sense. Support is given before, and afterwards something is received in exchange…
Well, it should not be like that. Support should ALWAYS be given afterwards; it should be given to those in command so that they command better, lead better. The counting should be done afterwards, strength should be measured afterwards, to see what can be done and to manage to get where one wants to go. Instead, with this system, “afterwards” the debts have to be paid, you have to give back to those who made you win, help those who helped you and so on. It is a chain that never ends until everything sinks into an abyss. It is as antithetical as can be imagined to the common good. It is antithetical to and distant from any possibility of growth; it is a system that exploits growth and speculates on it, but that can in no case make a contribution to the common good or to development …
