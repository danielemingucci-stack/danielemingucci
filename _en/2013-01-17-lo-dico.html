… I will stick my neck out. Bersani does not have much in the way of balls.

Already at the fall of Berlusconi in 2011 he should have taken command, never mind responsibility. A majority loses, an opposition rises: that is the pattern. Technocrats are used, they are not put in government. And that was the first occasion of timidity, of weakness. You have won: cash in. Instead he hid behind Monti and even behind Casini.

Now, looking to 2013, he is first, he was first by a mile, it was no contest. But he is doing everything not to win and not to find himself alone in command… Is he afraid of winning, of being alone in command? At party level, at local level, he knows how to play the games and how to win, but on the big target, on the real target, he does not put his balls on the table. Occhetto underestimated his opponent; here, instead, they are working on the resurrection of their opponents.

I fear he is not the right man, if this is the attitude. Let us imagine him in Europe…
