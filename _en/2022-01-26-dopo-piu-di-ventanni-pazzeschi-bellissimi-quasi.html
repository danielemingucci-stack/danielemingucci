After more than twenty crazy, wonderful years (almost 23), I am taking a diagonal step: in mid-December I spoke with Marco and Sapo, and on the first of February I will leave Empresa Creativa.

Creativity, communication, storytelling and the new languages, design and the passion for innovation: a thousand suggestions are drawing me towards something new. The adventures of these years have opened me up to an “advanced” vision of what I can do for my travelling companions, and I want to go a little further than traditional agency work. The new media, languages in continuous evolution and the rhythms “imposed” by the social network platforms give us the chance to start reasoning in a different, innovative, integrated way. And in a context of this kind, where digitalisation and quality of content must live together effectively, I want to make a positive contribution and make available the experience I have gained right in the field of innovation (I have dozens of patents!) and in that of communication and storytelling. I want to collaborate on branding projects and on enterprises (what a wonderful name) in which identity and the creation of content can truly express the potential at stake. And then there is Stramba, which from my point of view is perfectly organic to the work of communication, given that it is a challenge towards a new “language” in sailing, where comfort and performance can express potential still entirely to be discovered.
