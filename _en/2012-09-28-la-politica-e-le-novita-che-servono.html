I read a fine short article by Davide Rondoni in Clandestino Zoom about #Renzi. And it is so: he is the only new thing, and for that reason opposed by the professionals of his own party as well as, but this is more obvious, by the politically hostile sides.
One step is missing in the article, which is certainly obvious, but perhaps prevents going deeper: why is novelty needed? At what level is it needed? Is Renzi new at the right level, or is he just new?
The levels are many and on all of them the new is needed. But above all I believe it is the quality of administration that asks for something more, asks for much more than has been seen so far.
The newspapers are bringing to bear against politicians, and politics more generally, all the frustrations of a people, ours, that probably arrived unprepared for the challenge of the global market. No, not unprepared; perhaps it is fairer to say little aware of itself: now we are confronted not only with distant markets but also with distant ideas, almost alien conceptions of life, and we are distracted, reeling from the blows that all this chaotic “new” inevitably deals us. We who are instead exporters by election, we who are predestined for export… programmed to be a model and to be looked at.
Italy is the cradle of taste and style, good taste and fine style: what is needed is good administration and freedom. No other novelty is needed, with all due respect to those who hope to scrape together more funds for this or that (culture, social services, school, health…) and are really cultivating only the secret yearning to get out of the crisis in their own little professional world. With all due respect to those who hope to be funded because they got into the boat with the first, what is needed is only good administration and freedom, so as to generate, or rather regenerate, that humus in which culture, social services, school, health and so on can flower and excel. Italian style.
