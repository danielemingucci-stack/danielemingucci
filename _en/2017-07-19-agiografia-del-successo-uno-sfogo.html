It must be the habit of luxury, and they even allow themselves the luxury of talking about rebellion, but they do not know what they are saying. From the heights of success, of having made it, and of who knows what secrets (or abilities, heaven forbid). But someone who rebels is no strategist, no tactician. He is someone who goes off to die, someone like the devil who says fuck everything, we do it my way. Would anyone really give a toss about them if they had not been successful? Fuck, my motto is that if they put the effort in, everyone manages: to be cool you have to manage without putting the effort in, without toiling. McEnroe, not Roger Federer! He would not go and play the Australian Open because… fuck, it is in the middle of nowhere! Who can be bothered!
Success is a narrative con, because whoever tells it always plays the same little game. Ninety-nine times out of ten they do not tell the whole of it (read McEnroe’s book and see whether you understand why he was so good), but even if they do tell the whole of it, underneath it always comes out that, well, they deserved it, it was destiny, they were cut out for it, with the relative moral cooked to measure. And I, instead, think like Vasco Rossi, that nobody is different, that nobody smiles when they have lost, and I would like to see them, wise and sure with the uncertainties everyone has and they pretend not to have. Sour grapes? Not a bit! What the fuck do I care, I am the coolest in the world: when you throw yourself into a brawl, do you know what the secret is? Being willing to take a beating. You throw yourself into a scrum to take blows, not to give them. And who goes to take his dose of a hiding? Someone who feels fine with himself? No, someone who thinks he deserves it.
Men are like that; successful entrepreneurs I do not know, but they are deluding themselves if they think that keeping only the best is enough. In my opinion with children it is not enough; they know you, and where will teaching them to lie lead them? They have to remember my off-the-wall outbursts and decide whether to be only like me or much better than me, poor or rich, what the fuck do I care: they are the coolest in the world. Like Dad.
