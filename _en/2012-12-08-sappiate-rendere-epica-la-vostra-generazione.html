Know how to make your generation epic. Education is present relationship. It is now, it is not for later. Relationship present now. Living epically, and that is knowing as high the relationship with the mystery of time. Let us know how to experience the poetic and tragic (or comic, by God!) epos of our present! Let us know that the high relationship of the now is what gives us dignity in the eternal. Dignity of the eternal. With whom am I in relationship now? Against whom am I measuring myself? How high is my mission, even in the poverty of my gestures and of my possibilities? Epos, myth, generation, memory: all this is born from the awareness of a relationship with the Eternal. Awareness. Educating is developing this awareness and handing it down. Experiencing this awareness and living. As if every day were the last… NO! Living every day as if it were only the beginning!
