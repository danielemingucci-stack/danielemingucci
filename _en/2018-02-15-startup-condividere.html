In a startup, sharing, and not just accepting but promoting teamwork and the merging of skills, is part of the professionalism required (and indispensable); it is not an extra, perhaps tied to temperament. Limiting yourself to doing your own bit is simply not professional. With all due respect to those who think of professionalism as competence and experience.

Doing a startup is leaping into the void, and to do it you need vision, you need doubts, hypotheses and theories. Above all you need someone beside you who, at their best, tries to imagine with you… what effect it has.

What if…?
