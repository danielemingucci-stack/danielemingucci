The darkness of the night and that of the tunnel are completely different. They are not even alike. That of the night is gentle, that of the tunnel is harsh… There is as much difference as between happiness and pain… The darkness of the night opens, the darkness of the tunnel closes…

Tu Tung Chen Sung
