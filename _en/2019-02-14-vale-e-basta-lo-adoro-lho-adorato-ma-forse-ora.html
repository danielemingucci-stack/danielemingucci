Vale, and that’s enough…

I adore him, I have adored him, but perhaps that is enough now. Perhaps the MotoGP of monuments loses the charm of the swagger, of the Fenati-style mad turns, and becomes too wise, too Alain Prost and too little Mansell… perhaps it is time to say stop, to run a championship for the legends, to shorten the interviews and give the good bikes to the madmen fresh out of the egg rather than to certainties and monuments.
