You have to touch your own limit, push beyond it, not accept it. You cannot present yourself with the past, with your history, with who you are. Who you are is not who you will be; you have to do something new, more beautiful, greater than before. I do not know when it will be time to say enough, but as long as I can I have to grow. And I think that when it is no longer a more of quantity, beauty and greatness will change quality and will be more still. I cannot imagine the future with other aspirations… that is how I am made, I do not look back to understand who I am. I look beside me, if anything, and I look ahead; I see who is with me, I will see who will be there. There, this is a more intriguing measure, one that corresponds more to the cry of my heart.
Again.
