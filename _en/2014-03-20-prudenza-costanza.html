I do not have detailed documentation and I do not intend to get any, but the 20 lines of interview with Costanza Miriano in today’s La Voce di Romagna confirm me in my instinctive prejudice. She lives Christianity with a sense of superiority that is irritating, to say the least. Then from there, from the pulpit she has built herself, everything else follows by stringent logic. One can stand in solidarity with her over the attacks she has suffered, one can rightly demand that she too, with her personal extremism, should have the room to speak that is everyone’s right, but on the rest she is indefensible. I, and I have no intention of hiding it, have an instinctive dislike for someone who goes to the Pope and, instead of listening, talks, but the question goes far beyond that: it is Christianity lived as an awareness that becomes a capacity for judgement and then judgement exercised. Then a theory about the world, morality and so on… And so on, that is, goodbye: from the winged heights of this “higher consciousness” there is something for everyone: everything is judged and one feels better. No, of course, nobody will ever say “better” explicitly: the thing is disguised as “luckier”, graced, chosen etc…
I am sorry, but the “pretty, self-satisfied little woman” is not to my liking. A Littizzetto of the opposite sign, nothing more…
