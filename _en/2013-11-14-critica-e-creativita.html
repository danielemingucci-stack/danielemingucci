There are two distinct and profoundly different moments: that of critical discussion and that of creative production. Both have a value to express, but while the second can only be positive, the first can easily not be. Conversely, though, if for the creative moment it may not be easy to understand how much and what can be done better, the yardstick for the critical moment is simpler: if it opens things up it is useful, if it closes them down it is not.
