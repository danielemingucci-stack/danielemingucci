The fault of engineers focused only on maximum optimisation? Perhaps afraid of not reaching the performance they wanted, they lost sight of the project as a whole and… out comes the eyesore. Back then, how much did that weigh on the failure to convince the market? Swallowing a Prius was truly hard work (and it was only a hybrid), but there was far worse. Then someone began to make them beautiful, high-performing, and so on and so forth. Let us come to the Ferrari Luce. You do that design, you make a five-seater, you make it two point three tonnes… you come out on the very day Montezemolo is already with the journalists for Confindustria. At half a million and change…

Could it be that someone has built themselves an expensive excuse to abort electric for good?
