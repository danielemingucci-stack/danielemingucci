When children watch, respectful and a little bored, with a question mark of docile impatience that is already a judgement, open but clear-cut, hard… what shows through is the demand that life be more, somehow, even than the sacred we impose on them or simply propose. They really want more, and if it is not enough they get bored… They are alive.
