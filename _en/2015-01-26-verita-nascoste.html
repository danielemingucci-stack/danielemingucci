A truth for everyone is not a good truth. Many seem to think so. As if, in order to be interesting, to truly correspond, it could not be for everyone. A “successful” truth is not a truth that will do, in short, for those who feel in the right only when they also feel nonconformist. Too simple, too popular? There must be something behind it. Yes, calling a spade a spade is fine, but…

It is a kind of instinct that governs these sensations, a je-ne-sais-quoi that creates situations, looks for trouble behind every half sentence and cannot manage to enjoy either the positive or the moment.

I have noticed lately that you can be as Christian as you like, but as soon as you lift a finger you find someone more Christian than you who tells you how you should have done it, that you should have done more, that you should have gone in another direction. It happened to Benigni - it is funny to say so - it happens continually even to the pope. Antonio Socci, for example, is much more Christian than the pope…

Oh well, Socci is on another level, his daughter was raised from the dead, like Saint Catherine and Jesus Christ, but there are many who would know what the pope ought to say, or the leader of such-and-such a movement, or even Benigni (perhaps it should not have been written in that order). It may well be so, but…

Asserting your own “height” by marking a distance is not something I like. Wanting to be above, always, has for me a simple truth hidden inside it: fear.

A few years ago I spent some time with Father Pigi Bernareggi at Bertinoro, near Cesena. We were below the fortress, beautiful, imposing, on a marvellous day. At a certain point he pointed precisely at that fortress: “There, look how beautiful: our civilisation is like that…”. I paused to look at it; it really is beautiful, what a brilliant reading! Then Pigi turned to the opposite side: “… Christianity, instead, is like this”. He was pointing at the boundless view you see from up there, the plain the hills the sea, vast, lost, infinite. Well, I had fallen in with both feet, but this was written on my heart: beautiful is not enough, great is not enough, not even right or good… Infinite is enough, and the infinite is a relationship, a curiosity, an unpredictable dialogue. It is not only an assertion, because that would not be enough…

Pigi is someone who is not afraid of having a heart like other people’s. Those who are afraid of being the same are afraid of not being enough… they are probably looking inside themselves for something that is outside instead. Right outside, on the other shore, an old priest used to suggest many years ago… But the unexpected, it seems, is no longer in fashion. A real pity.
