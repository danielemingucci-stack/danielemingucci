without lifting their heads

without hearing the voice of Dylan

who shouts as he sings

how true their pain is.

The steps in a row

one after another

head down and thoughts

that do not know where to fly.

Music,

music is needed

otherwise those lost

gazes get lost again

and no one understands how

deep their story is.

They walk with their eyes down

as if there were only the ground

to bounce onto their faces

the light of the sky.

They are true perfect immense

they know each other without knowing it

and they wear the same gaze

the identical love, the same destiny.

They are last

at the bottom of the list of every thought

they shoot past me

fast as the perfect trajectories

of a heart that runs

And yet they are me

I am them and there is nothing more to say

there is no asynchronous beat

destiny calling

that could keep us apart

A moment longer.
