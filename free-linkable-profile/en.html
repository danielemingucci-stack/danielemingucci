Free Linkable Profile

A professional profile like the ones you know, but on your own site. It gets found by search engines and recognises other profiles of the same kind, with no platform in between.

Download the kit See a profile Read the specification

How it works

1

The profile is a page of your site

Cover, photo, headline, experience, education, skills: the structure is the one everybody knows how to read. The colours and fonts are yours.

2

People find you by your name

The page carries the data search engines use for people: first and last name, role, organisations, your other profiles.

3

Profiles recognise each other

Add the address of the profile of someone you know. When the other person adds yours too, both pages show "Mutual connection". The pages check it themselves, by reading each other.

4

Anyone who wants can follow you

The Follow button leads to the feed of what you publish, or to your newsletter.

Even without a site of your own, even on several sites

Hosting

Anyone with a site can host the profiles of people who don't have one: a company for its staff, an association for its members, a friend. Each person has their own page and address, and there is a list of the people hosted.

Showcase

The same profile can be shown on a second site. The showcase reads the data from the original on every visit, so it updates itself, and it states which one is the original.

Moving

The profile is a file: you take it wherever you like. A notice stays at the old address, and the profiles of the people connected to you update your address by themselves. A move counts only if both the old address and the new one confirm it.

Installing it by hand

1. Fill in profile.json

It is the only file you write: your data, in the open JSON Resume format.

2. Run python3 build.py

It generates the index.html page. No libraries needed.

3. Upload four files to your site

index.html, profile.json, flp.css, flp.js, in a folder such as /profile/.

In the kit

profile.json · the data build.py · the generator flp.css · the styling flp.js · the recognition SPEC.en.md · the specification

Free code, MIT licence. The kit's README is in Italian, with a short English summary; the labels of the profile switch to English with "lang": "en".

Built on open standards

JSON Resume for the data, schema.org and h-card to be read, rel="me" and rel="contact" links for the ties. Nothing proprietary.
